Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

4News is currently a static website. It does not require an account and does not ask readers to submit personal information to read news briefs.

Local settings

The site stores selected language, font, font size, colour theme, 4News commentary display preference, and people-link display preference in the browser's local storage. These settings stay on the reader's device and are used to restore preferences on future visits.

Analytics and logs

The current site does not include a first-party analytics script. Hosting providers, browsers, networks, or infrastructure services may still process standard technical logs such as IP address, user agent, request time, and requested URL.

Future features

If 4News adds email subscriptions, accounts, push notifications, advertising, or analytics, this policy should be updated before those features are enabled.

Contact

Contact details should be added before commercial launch so readers can ask privacy questions or request corrections.
