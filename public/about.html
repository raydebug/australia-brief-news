About 4news

Last updated: 29 July 2026

4news is an AI-powered Australian news briefing site. It collects publicly available signals from multiple news sources, groups related reports, and rewrites them into concise summaries for fast reading and listening.

The product is designed for readers who want a minimal view of what happened, why it matters, and where to read the original reporting.

What 4news does

  • Groups related Australian news reports into a single brief.
  • Links back to the original sources for context and verification.
  • Highlights differences between sources when multiple sources cover the same story.
  • Supports multiple interface and briefing languages.

What 4news does not do

  • It does not copy full article text, publisher images, video, audio, or official logos.
  • It does not replace the original reporting.
  • It does not provide legal, financial, medical, or professional advice.

Summaries are AI-generated from public sources and may contain errors. Readers should check original sources for full context.
