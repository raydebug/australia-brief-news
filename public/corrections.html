Corrections

Last updated: 29 July 2026

4News should correct or remove summaries that are materially inaccurate, misleading, outdated in a harmful way, or incorrectly attributed.

What to report

  • Incorrect facts, names, dates, locations, numbers, or attributions.
  • Summaries that overstate what original sources reported.
  • Incorrect grouping of unrelated stories.
  • Broken or wrong original source links.
  • Content that should be removed for legal, safety, or privacy reasons.

What to include

A useful correction report should include the news headline, the issue, the original source URL if available, and the correction requested.

Contact

A public correction contact should be added before commercial launch. Until then, this page documents the correction process that the site should follow.
