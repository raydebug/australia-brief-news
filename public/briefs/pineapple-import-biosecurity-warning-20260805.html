Pineapple import plan draws biosecurity warnings from growers

Queensland pineapple growers are pushing back against a federal proposal to open the market to fruit from India and Indonesia. Their concern is not only competition, but whether extra imports could raise biosecurity risks if pests or diseases get through inspection. It matters because Australia's fruit industries rely on strict quarantine rules and on trust in local supply. The decision now sits with agriculture officials and the import review process.
