Labor widens rooftop solar support for businesses

The federal government is expanding its solar discount scheme so larger commercial rooftop systems can qualify, not just small installations. Officials say a medium-sized business could save about $68,000 on installation, while a larger warehouse or factory could save about $232,000 up front and trim annual power bills by about $175,000. The move matters because it targets the part of the market that has lagged behind household rooftop solar, especially factories, retailers and logistics sites. The next question is whether approvals and installation capacity can keep pace.
