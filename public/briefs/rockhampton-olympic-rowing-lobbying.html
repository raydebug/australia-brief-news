Olympic rowing plans were pushed toward Rockhampton

Documents show lobbying to move 2032 Olympic rowing away from Brisbane and toward Rockhampton on the Fitzroy River. That matters because venue choices affect regional investment, transport planning and the political fight over whether the course is practical or symbolic. The main uncertainty is whether Queensland will keep the plan alive or stick with a different site.

Source differences
