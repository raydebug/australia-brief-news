Alan Jones's Sydney trial begins after years of legal wrangling

Former broadcaster Alan Jones has gone before a Sydney court to face 22 indecent assault and sexual touching charges tied to six complainants and alleged conduct between 2003 and 2020. The case matters because it is one of Australia's most high-profile criminal proceedings, it is expected to run for months, and it will test how much of the evidence and reporting reaches the public before a verdict. Jones denies the allegations and the next milestone is the full hearing in the local court.
