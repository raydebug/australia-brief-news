Court data is undercutting Labor's case for a new workplace court

Federal court figures suggest workplace and underpayment cases are already moving faster, which weakens Labor's argument for a new Fair Work Court. The government is still backing the plan after pressure from unions and supporters inside the party. The real test now is whether the change would speed up disputes or simply add another layer to an already busy system.
