Queensland boot camp plan triggers child safety warnings

Queensland welfare and legal groups are warning that the state's proposed youth boot camps could leave harm hidden from authorities. The plan would send some children as young as 10 to remote properties, and operators could claim a self-incrimination excuse instead of reporting abuse. That matters because the first camp is supposed to open by September if the legislation passes, and the final safeguards are still being debated in parliament. The bill has not yet cleared the state parliament, so the scheme is not locked in.
