Western Australia dirt-bike collision throws two riders into the air

Two riders were thrown through the air after a dirt bike hit a car in Western Australia, with the impact caught on camera. The story matters because it shows how suddenly a road crash can turn violent and how serious the injuries could have been. Police will now examine the collision, the riders' condition and whether any offence needs to be pursued.
