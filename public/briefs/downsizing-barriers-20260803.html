Older Australians still face barriers to downsizing

Many Australians over 55 say they are open to moving into a smaller home, but practical barriers keep slowing the shift. The biggest obstacles are emotional attachment, scarce suitable stock and the cost and paperwork of moving. It matters because unlocking more downsizer-friendly homes could ease pressure across a tight housing market. The next step is whether planning and tax settings make smaller homes easier to find and move into.
