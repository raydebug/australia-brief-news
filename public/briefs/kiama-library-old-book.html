Kiama library gets a book back after about 150 years

A book borrowed from Kiama Library roughly 150 years ago has been returned after it was found inside a bricked-up fireplace during home renovations. ABC adds that the volume will now be moved into a special collection, while the Guardian report focused on how extraordinary the overdue return is. The story matters because it turns a local curiosity into a conservation job, and the next step is preserving the book for public access rather than lending it out again.
