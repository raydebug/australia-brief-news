Payne stretches his Supercars lead with a Perth win

Matt Payne won the opening race of the Perth Super 440 at Wanneroo Raceway after a late safety car tightened the field. Brodie Kostecki's race unravelled when he spun out in a battle with Kai Allen, while Ryan Wood and Anton De Pasquale finished behind Payne on the podium. The result lifts Payne to a 118-point championship lead and secures Ford the manufacturers' title for 2026. The rest of the weekend will show whether anyone can claw back ground.
