Australians are being warned about a Census door-knocking scam

Australians are being told to watch out for door-knocking scams ahead of the August 11 Census. The warning is aimed at people who might be asked for bank details, tax file numbers or passwords by impostors. It matters because Census data is used for funding, planning and public services, so trust in the process has to be protected. The ABS says it will not ask for sensitive personal details in that way.
