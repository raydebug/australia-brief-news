Australian schools are being warned that posted photos can be turned into deepfakes

Schools across Australia are being told to think carefully before posting student and staff photos online because AI tools can reuse those images in harmful ways. The warning follows a surge in images being copied from school websites and social accounts, then altered into sexualised or abusive material. That matters because once a photo is public, it can be lifted and repurposed long after the original post is forgotten. The next step is whether schools tighten privacy settings and whether regulators push harder for clearer rules.
