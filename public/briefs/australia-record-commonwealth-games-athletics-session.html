Australia posts a record Commonwealth Games athletics session

Australia's track and field team delivered a record session in Glasgow, with the men's 4x100m relay won for the first time since 1974, Abbey Caldwell leading a sweep of the women's mile podium, Kurtis Marschall setting a Commonwealth Games record in the pole vault, and Matt Denny defending his discus title. The session matters because it showed Australian medals coming from sprints, middle distance and throws rather than a single event. It also pushed the country's gold tally higher heading into the closing days of the Games. The next question is whether Australia can keep converting finals into titles before the schedule ends.
