Labor moves to patch the widow-tax gap before the next tax tranche

Labor is moving to patch the tax settings that critics dubbed the widow tax, extending the promised CGT and negative-gearing carve-outs to cover death and divorce cases properly. The change matters because jointly owned investment properties can be caught by the gap when a relationship ends or a spouse dies. Treasury says consultation on the draft runs until 21 August, so the practical question is whether the fix is locked in before any vulnerable owners are hit by the old rules.
