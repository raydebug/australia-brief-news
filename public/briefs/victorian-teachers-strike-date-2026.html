Victorian teachers set another strike date as pay talks stall

Victorian public school teachers have set August 19 as the date for another 24-hour stoppage if the government does not return with a better pay offer. The dispute matters because it is now disrupting families and school planning across the state, and it comes after repeated industrial action this year. The next step depends on whether the new premier's team reopens negotiations in time to avert another shutdown.
