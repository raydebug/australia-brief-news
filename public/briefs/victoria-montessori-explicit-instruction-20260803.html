Victoria's explicit-instruction push is phasing out Montessori classes

A public school in Victoria is phasing out its Montessori stream as the state keeps pushing schools toward explicit instruction. Families have valued the program because it gives students a more flexible, self-directed way to learn, especially those dealing with health issues or anxiety. The issue matters because a statewide teaching model can crowd out alternative classroom approaches. The open question is whether the school can keep any form of the program, and whether more Victorian Montessori streams disappear before the 2028 deadline.
