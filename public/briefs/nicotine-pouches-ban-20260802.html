Australia tightens the clampdown on nicotine pouches

Australia has moved to stop nicotine pouches circulating through shops and online sellers. Officials say the products are easy to hide, easy to use and increasingly reaching young people, which makes the health risk harder to contain. The ban matters because it is aimed at stopping a new nicotine habit before it spreads further. The next test is whether enforcement, border checks and retailer compliance can keep the products out.
