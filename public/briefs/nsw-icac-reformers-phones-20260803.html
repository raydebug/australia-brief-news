NSW ICAC pressure grows as Reformers' tactics come under scrutiny

NSW's corruption watchdog is hearing evidence about a Liberal faction accused of illegal donation schemes and branch-stacking tactics. The immediate focus is on meetings, phone handling and political contacts that investigators say were used to avoid scrutiny and push the group's agenda. It matters because the inquiry could damage the NSW Liberal Party's leadership and credibility just months before an election. More witnesses are due this week, so the picture is still developing.
