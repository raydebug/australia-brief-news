Labor's news levy rewrite draws pushback from publishers

The federal government has rewritten its news bargaining levy so tech companies still face pressure to make deals with Australian publishers, but the tax now works on a narrower base. That matters because the change could still funnel money to local journalism while lowering the chance of a bigger trade fight. The open question is whether the revised plan is strong enough to keep large platforms negotiating.
