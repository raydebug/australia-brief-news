Ridge Barredo vows to keep lifting after a nasty Games injury

Australian weightlifter Ridge Barredo has suffered a knee injury after a failed lift in Glasgow, but he says the setback will not end his career. He was chasing a strong finish in the men's 110-kilogram class when the attempt went wrong and left him on crutches, with scans now due to check the damage. The story matters because one bad lift can turn a medal run into a rehab plan. The next step is medical assessment and recovery, while Barredo is already talking about coming back.
