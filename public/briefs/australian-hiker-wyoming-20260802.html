Australian hiker found dead after Wyoming fall

An Australian hiker, William Standish, has been found dead in Wyoming after going missing on the Continental Divide Trail. Reports say contact stopped after he last checked in with family, and investigators believe a fall on steep terrain is the likely cause. The case matters because it ends a search that drew in local rescuers and because it is a reminder of how dangerous remote long-distance hikes can be. Formal findings are still to come.
