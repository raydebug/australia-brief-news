Prestigious Perth school suspends students over antisemitic group chat

Wesley College in South Perth has suspended students after antisemitic language and memes were used in a private group chat to target another student. The case matters because it shows how quickly abuse can spread through private messaging and spill into school discipline. It also keeps pressure on schools to respond clearly to hate speech, even when it happens off campus. The next step is an investigation into the chat and the school's wider response.
