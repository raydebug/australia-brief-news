ABC investigations chief leaves as Four Corners scrutiny intensifies

Jo Puccini is leaving the ABC after a long career as the broadcaster continues reviewing the accuracy of some investigative programs. The exit comes as the ABC pulls back material and faces more questions about how its investigations unit handled contested reporting. It matters because this is a credibility issue for one of Australia's most visible public-interest newsrooms. The open question is whether the review leads to tighter editorial checks or broader staffing change.
