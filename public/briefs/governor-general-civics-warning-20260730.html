Sam Mostyn warns civic ignorance is eroding democracy

Governor-General Sam Mostyn says Australia's democracy is being weakened by poor civic understanding and by pressure on public institutions. Her warning matters because it links civic education to trust in government, not just school lessons. The next question is whether leaders treat that warning as a priority before public confidence drops further.
