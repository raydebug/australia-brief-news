Victorian teachers prepare another strike as pay talks stall

Victorian state-school teachers are heading for another 24-hour strike after the union said the latest pay offer still falls short. That matters because repeated stoppages disrupt families and show the dispute is still unresolved despite months of bargaining. If neither side shifts, the strike could go ahead on August 19 and keep pressure on the government to improve the offer.
