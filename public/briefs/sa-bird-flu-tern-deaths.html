South Australian tern deaths point to a worsening bird flu outbreak

South Australia is confronting a wider bird flu problem after dead and sick greater crested terns were found at Baudin Rocks and testing moved to confirm the strain. That matters because the virus is now showing up in native wildlife rather than staying at an early warning stage, which raises the risk to seabird colonies and other species. The next step is lab confirmation and close monitoring for any further spread.
