NSW corruption inquiry widens over Reformers branch-stacking claims

The NSW ICAC inquiry is examining whether Liberal factional figures used prohibited donations and branch-stacking tactics to build influence inside the party. The issue matters because it reaches into donor rules, internal party control and the credibility of senior political figures. The next stage is more evidence from the hearing, possible referrals and a clearer picture of whether the allegations become formal findings.
