Former Mildura businessman sentenced after shooting neighbour's dog

A former Mildura supermarket owner has been sentenced after pleading guilty to shooting his neighbour's shih tzu with an air rifle. Court records say John Green fired at the dog Otis multiple times at an Essendon unit and also breached firearm storage rules. It matters because the case combines animal cruelty with unsafe weapon handling, which the court treated as a serious offence. The sentence brings the case to a close unless there is an appeal.
