Australia's electric vehicle sales hit a new quarterly high

Australia's latest vehicle data point to a record quarter for electric and hybrid cars. Sales rose sharply as higher fuel prices and a wider mix of models pulled more buyers into the market, especially in family and plug-in categories. That matters because it shows the shift away from petrol-only cars is moving faster than many expected, even though charging access and upfront prices still shape decisions. The next question is whether this pace holds once fuel pressure eases.
