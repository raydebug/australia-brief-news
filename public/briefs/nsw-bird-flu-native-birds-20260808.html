NSW confirms its first native-bird bird flu cases

New South Wales has confirmed H5 bird flu in native birds, showing the virus has reached wildlife inside the state. The immediate concern is how far it may spread through wetlands, coastlines and inland waterways that moving birds share. The wider significance is that Australia is still trying to contain an outbreak that can escalate quickly once it gets into local populations. The next step is more surveillance and biosecurity checks to see whether this is a one-off detection or the start of broader spread.
