Labor's migration overhaul keeps drawing pressure from business and MPs

The government is still trying to reset migration settings by slowing net overseas migration while leaving the permanent intake target at 185,000. Tony Burke's delayed speech has become the focal point, because ministers are weighing tighter visa processing and how far to go without upsetting employers or the party room. This matters because migration, housing and labour shortages are all being argued at the same time. The next step is the rescheduled announcement and the policy detail behind it.
