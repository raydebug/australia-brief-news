Australian motorists face a sharp fuel price jump as excise relief ends

Drivers across Australia are heading into a higher-price period at the bowser as fuel excise relief ends and oil costs stay elevated. Reports point to petrol moving above $2 a litre in many capitals, with diesel also set to rise. It matters because the increase lands straight on household budgets, delivery costs and inflation expectations. The open question is how fast stations pass it on and whether drivers change their buying habits.
