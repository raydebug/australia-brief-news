Fitzroy Community School faces fresh abuse allegations

An alternative school in Melbourne is under fresh scrutiny after allegations that male students ranked female students and shared the material online, while separate abuse claims stretch back decades. The school has brought in an investigator as families and former students press for accountability. That matters because community-run schools still need strong safeguarding and complaint systems. The next step is the investigation and any follow-up from regulators or police.
