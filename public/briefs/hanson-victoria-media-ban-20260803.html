Hanson shuts major outlets out of her Victorian campaign launch

Pauline Hanson used a Melbourne event to unveil One Nation's Victorian election team while keeping ABC, The Age and The Guardian out of the room. The move matters because it shows the party is trying to control coverage as it builds for the November state election. The question now is whether the publicity helps One Nation gain momentum or simply deepens criticism of its media strategy.
