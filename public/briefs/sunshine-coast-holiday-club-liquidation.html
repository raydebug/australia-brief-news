Sunshine Coast holiday club collapses after refund complaints mount

A Sunshine Coast holiday business has entered liquidation after customer complaints about refunds and service continued to build. That matters because people who paid in advance now face a longer wait to see whether any money can be recovered. The liquidator will now work through assets, debts and any claims from customers and creditors.
