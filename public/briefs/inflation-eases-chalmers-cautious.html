Borrowers still feel the squeeze despite softer inflation

Australia's latest inflation data has eased some pressure on rate expectations, but banks are still reporting tight conditions for borrowers. New reporting says NAB has seen a sharp drop in home-loan applications, which suggests households are not yet getting much relief from the cost-of-living squeeze. The broader picture is that lower inflation has not translated into easier credit or cheaper repayments. The next few days will show whether lenders and the Reserve Bank keep softening their outlook.
