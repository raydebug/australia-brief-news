WA refinery plan moves ahead as governments examine fuel security

The federal and Western Australian governments are moving ahead with a feasibility study for a new oil refinery in WA. The proposal matters because Australia has lost most of its domestic refining capacity, which leaves the country exposed when global fuel markets tighten. The study will now test whether the project is affordable, what it would need to import, and whether it can meaningfully improve fuel security.
