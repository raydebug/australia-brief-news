A Sydney woman faces 62 charges over threatening letters

Police say a Sydney woman has been charged with 62 offences over letters allegedly sent to politicians, media organisations and religious groups. The investigation matters because it shows how a long-running pattern of threats can become a large criminal case once police connect the letters. The arrest followed a strike-force probe and a search of her home. The next step is the court process and any further evidence police say links the letters together.
