McDermott Aviation helicopters collide in Greece wildfire fight

Two firefighting helicopters leased by McDermott Aviation collided over Greece while crews were battling wildfires near Athens, killing two people on board one aircraft. The company says it is deeply saddened, and the surviving crew were able to land safely. It matters because Australian aircraft are part of a wider international fire response, and investigators now have to work out how the aircraft came together in the air.
