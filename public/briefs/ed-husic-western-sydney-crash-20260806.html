Ed Husic is on short leave after western Sydney crash

Labor MP Ed Husic was sent to hospital after a crash on Sydney's M7 left him with fractures and a concussion. He has since been discharged and is taking a short break from parliamentary duties while police continue to review the collision. The case matters because a senior federal MP is recovering from a serious road accident, and witnesses and dashcam footage are still being checked. He is expected back once doctors clear him.
