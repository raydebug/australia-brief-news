WA's FOGO bin rules are being tightened

Western Australia has tightened its FOGO bin rules, removing several items that householders used to put in green-lidded bins. The state says the change is needed to cut contamination and make composting work properly, but the timing has confused some councils and residents. That matters because waste systems only work if people know exactly what goes where. The next step is whether the new rules are explained clearly enough to avoid more contamination and complaints.
