Fatal Shark Bay crash leaves a holiday trip in tragedy

A crash near Shark Bay has left two men dead and two other passengers seriously injured after two utilities collided on the North West Coastal Highway. Police say the vehicles hit each other about 11:30am on Monday, then a woman in her 50s and a 12-year-old were flown to Perth for treatment. It matters because the road is remote and serious crashes there can quickly turn fatal before help arrives. Investigators now need to work out what caused the collision and whether speed, fatigue or road conditions played a part.
