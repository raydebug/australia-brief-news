North Sydney Olympic Pool reopens after a $122 million rebuild

North Sydney Olympic Pool has reopened after a five-year, $122 million rebuild that ran over time and over budget. The 1936 landmark now has upgraded pools and wellness facilities, and the first three days are free for the public. It matters because the project was both a civic asset and a long-running political headache. The question now is whether the finished site justifies the cost.
