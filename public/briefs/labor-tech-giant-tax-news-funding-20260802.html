Labor finalises a tech tax to push big platforms toward news funding

Labor has finalised a tax change that targets digital advertising revenue from large tech firms and is meant to support Australian journalism. The policy marks a shift in the bargaining fight between news publishers and global platforms. It matters because it could reshape how Australian media is funded and how much leverage the government has over the tech giants. The detail still matters, especially around who pays and how much money actually flows to newsrooms.
