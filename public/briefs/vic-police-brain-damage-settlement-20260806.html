Victoria Police settles a brain-injury lawsuit after a botched arrest

Victoria Police has settled a civil claim brought by a Gippsland father who was left with permanent brain damage after a bungled arrest in 2022. The reported payout is close to $14 million, making it one of the force's biggest recent settlements. It closes a case that raised questions about arrest tactics and police accountability. The settlement ends the civil fight, but it does not change the underlying concern about how the arrest was handled.
