Pauline Hanson pushes passport-blocking anti-ISIS bill

Pauline Hanson has put forward a bill that would let authorities refuse, suspend or cancel passports for Australians charged with or convicted of terrorism offences. The proposal also seeks tougher exclusion and bail powers, and Tony Burke says the plan is likely to run into constitutional limits. The story matters because it sits at the point where national security, citizenship rights and court oversight collide. The next question is whether the bill can survive legal scrutiny or becomes a political symbol.
