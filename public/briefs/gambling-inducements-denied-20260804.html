Betting firms deny claims of drug and escort inducements

A Senate inquiry into gambling reform heard claims that some betting companies had offered drugs, alcohol and escorts to keep high-value customers wagering. Sportsbet and Tabcorp rejected the allegations and said any evidence should be handed to authorities. The story matters because it feeds directly into the government's gambling ad reform push, while lawmakers weigh whether the current bill goes far enough to curb inducements and ads aimed at children. The inquiry is due to report on August 17, so the next step is whether the hearing leads to tighter rules or formal investigations.
