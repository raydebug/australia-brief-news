South Australia pushes ahead with its long-delayed truth-telling commission

South Australia is moving ahead with a truth-telling commission after years of discussion, with Indigenous leaders saying the process is needed to confront the state's history more honestly. The commission matters because it sets the framework for what can be heard publicly, how evidence is collected and whether reconciliation policy moves beyond symbolism. The next step is the formal rollout and the scope it is given.
