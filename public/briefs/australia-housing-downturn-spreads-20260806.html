Australia's housing downturn spreads beyond Sydney and Melbourne

Recent housing data show prices are sliding across more of Australia, with national values falling in July and the weakness spreading beyond Sydney and Melbourne. Analysts point to higher interest rates, tax changes affecting investors and a shift in market sentiment as the main drivers. It matters because housing sits at the centre of cost-of-living pressure, borrowing and election politics. The open question is whether the decline stays moderate or deepens into a bigger correction.
