NSW moves to implement Bondi Junction inquest reforms

NSW has agreed to adopt the recommendations from the Bondi Junction coronial inquest. The package includes a stronger health-led response for some mental health emergencies and other changes meant to improve how the state handles future attacks and crises. It matters because the findings were meant to turn the 2024 tragedy into lasting reform, not just a review on paper. The implementation phase now becomes the test, with some changes likely to take time and others needing budget and coordination.
