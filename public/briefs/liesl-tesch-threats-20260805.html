Alleged threats against MP Liesl Tesch lead to court

Police say a man is facing court over alleged online threats directed at Gosford MP and Paralympian Liesl Tesch. The case centres on messages said to have been sent through social media and gaming platforms, not an in-person confrontation. It matters because abuse aimed at public figures can quickly become a safety issue and can discourage people from taking part in public life. The next step is the court process and any further evidence about the messages.
