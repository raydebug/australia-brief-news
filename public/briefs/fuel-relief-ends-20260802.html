Fuel relief ends as motorists brace for higher pump prices

The temporary fuel excise relief has ended, and petrol and diesel prices are expected to rise over the coming days. That matters because households are already under pressure from higher transport costs and stubborn inflation. Drivers will see the change first, while retailers and policymakers watch how quickly the impact spreads.
