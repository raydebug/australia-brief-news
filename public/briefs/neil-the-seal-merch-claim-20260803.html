Neil the seal merchandise site is pulled over donation claims

The Neil the seal merchandise site has been taken down after questions over its donation claims and the artwork used on the store. That matters because a viral wildlife brand can quickly become a consumer-protection issue when charity promises are unclear. The next question is whether any regulator or platform action follows and whether the operators explain the missing partnership.
