Labor's family reunion delay plan is drawing human rights warnings

Migration lawyers and rights advocates are warning that Labor's plan to delay or narrow some family reunion pathways could become a human rights issue. The argument is about how long separated families may wait, who can apply and whether the system becomes harsher. The government has not locked in the final settings, so the political fight is still open.
