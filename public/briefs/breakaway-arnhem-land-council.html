Garma announcement opens the door to a new Arnhem Land council

At Garma Festival, Yolŋu leader Djawa Yunupiŋu announced plans for the Dilak Land Council, a new body for 13 clans in north-east Arnhem Land. The proposal would shift more land governance and development control toward Traditional Owners and away from the Northern Land Council. That matters because it could reshape how decisions are made across a major stretch of the Top End, on the 50th anniversary of the Aboriginal Land Rights Act. Formal approval and negotiations are still ahead, so the announcement is important but not final.
