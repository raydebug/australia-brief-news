Melbourne court sends ISIS-linked mother and daughter to trial

A Melbourne court has committed Kawsar Ahmad and her daughter Zeinab Ahmad to stand trial on slavery charges linked to their time in Syria. Prosecutors allege the family was tied to Islamic State and controlled a Yazidi teenager, and the case now moves to the Victorian Supreme Court with a hearing listed for August 21. The case matters because it shows Australian courts handling alleged crimes committed during the collapse of Islamic State territory. The next step is the pre-trial process, where the evidence and the charges will be tested.
