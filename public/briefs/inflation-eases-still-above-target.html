Inflation cools slightly, but rate relief is still uncertain

Australia's June inflation reading has eased to 3.8 per cent, which takes some pressure off the August rate decision but leaves prices well above the Reserve Bank's target band. The biggest costs still sitting high are housing, electricity and groceries, while cheaper fuel has helped soften the headline number. The practical question now is whether the RBA sees enough progress to hold rates, or whether stubborn underlying prices force it to stay cautious for longer.
