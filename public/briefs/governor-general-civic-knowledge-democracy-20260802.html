Governor-General says weak civic knowledge is damaging Australia's democracy

Governor-General Sam Mostyn says Australia is facing a growing democratic problem because many people do not understand the country's constitutional system. She argues that civic ignorance and abuse of public figures are discouraging participation, so she wants a stronger push on civics education and public trust. The next question is whether governments and schools turn that warning into policy.
