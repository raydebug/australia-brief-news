Sydney threats case ends in 62 charges

A Sydney woman has been denied bail after police laid 62 charges over alleged death threats sent to senior politicians and other targets. The case matters because it shows how repeated threats can quickly become a major police and court matter. It also keeps pressure on NSW police and prosecutors to act before intimidation spreads further. The next step is the court process and whether the allegations are tested in full.
