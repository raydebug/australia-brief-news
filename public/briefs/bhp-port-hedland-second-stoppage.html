BHP faces another full-day stoppage in Port Hedland

Workers at BHP's Port Hedland operations are set for another 24-hour stoppage on August 9 after unions escalated the pay dispute. The site is a critical iron-ore export hub, so even a short shutdown can affect shipments, royalties and bargaining leverage on both sides. The immediate question is whether BHP can keep exports moving while talks continue and whether the strike opens the door to wider industrial action.
