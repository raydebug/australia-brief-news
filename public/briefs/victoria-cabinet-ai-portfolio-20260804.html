Ben Carroll adds an AI portfolio to Victoria's new ministry

Victoria's new premier, Ben Carroll, has named a fresh ministry and created a dedicated portfolio for ethical artificial intelligence. The move matters because it puts AI policy inside cabinet rather than leaving it as an afterthought, while also signalling a reset after the leadership change. The next questions are which department will run the work, how much authority the new minister gets and whether the reshuffle calms the government's internal turbulence.
