More first-home deposit purchases are ending up as investment properties

New data shows nearly 1,500 homes bought through Australia’s 5 per cent first-home deposit scheme have since been turned into investment properties. The figures matter because the program was designed to help owner-occupiers, yet some higher-income buyers have been able to use it while house prices and policy settings shift. One of the most striking examples was a couple on $674,000 a year, and the next question is whether Canberra tightens the scheme.
