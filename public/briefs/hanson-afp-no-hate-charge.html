Police rule out hate charge over Hanson comments

Police have decided Pauline Hanson's remarks about Muslims do not meet the threshold for a hate-speech case. The decision matters because it keeps the One Nation leader at the centre of the migration and social cohesion debate, and the comments drew complaints from Muslim groups and criticism from across politics. The open question is whether the finding ends the issue or fuels another round of political attacks.
