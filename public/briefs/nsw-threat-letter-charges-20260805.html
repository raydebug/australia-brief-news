NSW police charge a woman over threatening letters

NSW police have charged a woman with 62 offences after a long-running investigation into threatening letters sent to politicians, media outlets and religious groups. The case matters because it shows how harassment can move from inboxes to postal letters and put public figures, staff and community groups under sustained pressure. Police still need to work through the wider trail of material and any links to earlier complaints. The next step is the court process and whether investigators identify any broader pattern.
