Australian booksellers warn AI demand is destroying rare titles

Australian booksellers say a new AI-driven market for old books is putting rare and out-of-print titles at risk. The concern is that bulk buyers may be sending books through destructive scanning workflows, where spines are cut, pages are digitised and the originals are discarded. It matters because that would strip cultural and historical value out of physical collections, not just their text. The next step is whether buyers, publishers and regulators can prove where these books are going and stop any unnecessary destruction.
