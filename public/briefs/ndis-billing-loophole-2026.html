NDIS billing loophole exposes widespread overcharging of disability supports

The federal government says some NDIS providers have been charging far above expected rates, with one provider allegedly billing more than $1 million and thousands of others also using the wrong category. The issue matters because the scheme now funds a huge share of disability services, so even small billing gaps add up quickly. Canberra is responding by tightening price settings and oversight, but the question is whether the new rules will stop misuse without cutting off legitimate supports.
