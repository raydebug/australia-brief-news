Brisbane's homelessness count climbs past 1,500 children

Brisbane Zero's latest snapshot says 984 families, including 1,539 children, were identified as homeless across the city in the last financial year. Most of those families were headed by single mothers, which shows how quickly housing stress turns into family displacement. The figure matters because it makes the city's housing crisis measurable and local, not abstract, and it points to pressure on emergency accommodation, schools and support services. The next step is whether governments and service groups turn that count into more stable housing pathways.
