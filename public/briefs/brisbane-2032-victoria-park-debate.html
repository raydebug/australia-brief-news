Brisbane unveils Victoria Park masterplan as 2032 debate continues

Queensland has released a draft Victoria Park masterplan for the Brisbane 2032 precinct, setting out the stadium footprint, new connections, green space and public areas around the site. Public consultation is now open and runs into late September. It matters because Victoria Park is the headline piece of Brisbane's Olympic legacy and will shape the inner city long after the Games. The next step is to see whether feedback from residents and heritage concerns can be folded into the final design without blowing out the delivery timetable.
