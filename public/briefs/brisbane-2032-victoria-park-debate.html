Brisbane's 2032 precinct plans renew Victoria Park debate

Queenslanders are being asked to weigh in on the 2032 Olympic plans as new concept images for the Victoria Park precinct renew scrutiny of the venue choice. The proposal keeps most of the park public, but critics say the design still threatens green space and cultural sites. It matters because the Olympic footprint will shape Brisbane's inner city for decades and decide how much of the park survives intact. The next step is consultation and further design work before any final approval.
