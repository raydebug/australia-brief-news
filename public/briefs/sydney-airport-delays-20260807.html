Sydney Airport is hit by widespread delays after controller staffing shortages

Sydney Airport faced major delays and cancellations after Airservices Australia imposed flow controls because too few controllers were available. Flights on Qantas, Jetstar, Virgin and Rex were affected, with some passengers stuck on the tarmac for up to two hours. It matters because the disruption hit a critical transport hub and showed how thin staffing can quickly spread through the national network. Airservices says the restrictions have been lifted, but the next test is whether rosters and staffing are strengthened before the next crunch.
