Sydney motorway toll cuts are on the way

Several Sydney toll roads are set for lower charges under a new New South Wales agreement. The move matters because tolls have become a major living-cost pressure in the city, and the government is trying to ease that burden without blowing out the transport budget. The savings will depend on which roads people use most, and the rollout still needs to be locked in.
