Three family members die in a Wiley Park house fire

A fire at a Wiley Park granny flat in Sydney's south-west has left three people dead, including a 16-year-old boy. Police say crews were called about 9:45pm on Wednesday and found the victims after forcing their way through the burning rear dwelling. Investigators from NSW Police and Fire and Rescue NSW are still working to identify the bodies and determine how the blaze started. The scene has been declared a crime scene, and a coroner's report will follow.
