Remote WA caravan crash kills two and leaves child critical

A crash in remote Western Australia killed two people and left a child fighting for life after a vehicle towing a caravan rolled near Shark Bay. The child was flown to hospital by the Royal Flying Doctor Service. The case matters because remote crashes can turn deadly quickly, and investigators now need to determine exactly how the rollover happened.
