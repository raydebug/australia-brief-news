Perth doctor faces fresh child sex charges

A Perth paediatrician has been charged with another 15 offences, bringing the case back into the spotlight in Western Australia. The allegations involve alleged sexual offending against children and have widened an already serious criminal matter. It matters because police and the courts are now dealing with more alleged victims and a larger evidence picture. The next stage will depend on what is tested in court and whether more charges follow.
