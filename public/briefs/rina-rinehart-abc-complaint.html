Rinehart seeks removal of a satirical ABC segment

One ABC Race Around the World segment has triggered a formal complaint from Gina Rinehart's company after a satirical line about her went to air. The complaint is asking for the clip to be removed, for an apology and for the approval process to be reviewed, while the broadcaster says its internal complaints process is still under way. It matters because the dispute tests the boundary between satire and legal pressure at a public broadcaster. The next step is the Ombudsman's review and any response from ABC management or regulators.
