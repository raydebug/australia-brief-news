Perth jury clears Yung Filly of rape but leaves retrial possible

A Perth jury has acquitted Yung Filly of the rape charges linked to a 2024 hotel encounter after his Perth show, but it found him guilty of two assault counts and could not reach a verdict on three other rape charges. The case matters because it has now shifted from the trial itself to sentencing and the chance of a retrial on the unresolved counts. The next step is a court mention set for later in August, when prosecutors will decide whether to pursue those remaining charges again.
