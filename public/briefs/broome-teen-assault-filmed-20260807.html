Broome assault filmed by teens puts police response in focus

A former police officer says she and a friend were attacked by a group of teenage girls while riding in Broome, and the assault was filmed. The case has raised fresh concern about violence in remote WA and how police handle reports like this. The next step is the response from investigators and whether charges are laid.
