Petrol prices are set to rise again as fuel relief ends

The temporary fuel discount is ending on August 3, and drivers are being warned that petrol prices will keep climbing, with some cities already above $2 a litre. That matters because the change will hit household budgets and freight costs just as inflation is still being watched closely. The next question is how quickly retailers pass through the new excise rate and whether officials can stop any sharp price gouging.
