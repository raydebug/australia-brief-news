Harvey Norman and Latitude are ordered to pay $55 million over misleading finance ads

The Federal Court has ordered Harvey Norman and Latitude to pay a combined $55 million after a finance promotion was found to have misled shoppers. The case matters because it shows how quickly interest-free marketing can become a consumer-law problem when extra credit-card steps and fees are hidden. Both companies now have to publish corrective notices while the regulator watches for wider fallout.
