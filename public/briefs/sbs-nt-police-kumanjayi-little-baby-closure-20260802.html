NT police say the Little Baby discovery brought some closure

Northern Territory police say the discovery of Kumanjayi Little Baby has brought some closure after a long and painful search. The case matters because it sits at the intersection of grief, policing and trust in remote communities. The next step is supporting the family and clarifying what investigators can responsibly say about how the case unfolded.
