Darwin's live music circuit is losing touring acts

Music venues and performers in Darwin say touring acts are skipping the Top End more often, leaving fewer headline shows and less income for local workers. Artists and venue operators say the problem is not just entertainment: it affects jobs, audience turnout and whether the city can keep a reliable live circuit. That matters because remote capitals rely on touring schedules to stay culturally connected and commercially viable. What happens next depends on whether support or booking incentives can fill the gap.
