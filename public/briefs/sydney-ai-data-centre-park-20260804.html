Sydney AI data centre plan triggers parkland backlash

A proposed redevelopment of the former Kogarah Golf Club site in Arncliffe has drawn strong local objections because an AI data centre is listed among possible uses. The broader plan includes logistics, commercial space, an hotel, parking and public parkland along the Cooks River. The practical issue is whether Sydney can expand digital infrastructure without sacrificing scarce urban open space, local amenity and grid capacity. The next step is the NSW planning assessment and the applicant response to community submissions.
