Rising costs shut a Queensland mushroom farm after 45 years

A Queensland mushroom-growing family says rising operating costs have made the business unsustainable. After 45 years, it is shutting rather than absorbing more losses. The broader issue is how energy, labour and supply prices keep squeezing regional food producers, and whether more farms follow suit.
