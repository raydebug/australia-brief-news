Noel Pearson pushes a reset for Indigenous governance at Garma

Noel Pearson used Garma to argue for dismantling state Aboriginal affairs departments and shifting more responsibility to the Commonwealth. His speech landed as Yolŋu leaders moved to create a new land council in north-east Arnhem Land, underlining a broader push for greater Indigenous control over decisions on country. It matters because the debate goes to land rights, service delivery and who sets policy for remote communities. The next question is whether governments treat it as a serious reform agenda or another festival speech.
