Some bushfire-prone homeowners are spending tens of thousands to stay insured

In parts of Australia exposed to bushfire risk, homeowners are spending large sums on clearing work, home upgrades and other mitigation measures just to keep their insurance options open. The SBS report focuses on residents near bushland north-west of Sydney who say they have already done what they can, yet premiums remain punishing. That matters because rising insurance costs can lock families into risky properties or make it harder to prepare properly for fire season. The open question is whether insurers will start recognising verified risk reduction more fairly.
