WA flu jab campaign looks set to miss its target

Western Australia is now expected to fall short of its flu vaccination target as free jabs wind down. That matters because lower uptake leaves more people exposed as winter illness keeps circulating, especially older residents and people with health risks. The next question is whether health officials add another push or accept that the campaign has already peaked too late to move the numbers.
