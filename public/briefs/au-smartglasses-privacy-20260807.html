Smart glasses put pressure on Australia's privacy rules

Australia's privacy debate is shifting toward camera-equipped smart glasses after officials warned they can record people without clear consent. The concern is that bystanders can be filmed in public before they notice, especially women and children. That matters because the current rules were built before cheap wearable cameras became common. The next step is whether Canberra tightens privacy law or issues new guidance as complaints and public unease grow.
