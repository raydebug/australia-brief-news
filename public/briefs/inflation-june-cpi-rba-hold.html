Softer June inflation gives the RBA room to pause, but the pressure has not gone away

Australia's June inflation reading came in at 3.8 per cent, with trimmed mean inflation at 3.6 per cent. That eased the immediate case for another Reserve Bank rate rise, but household costs are still being pushed up by housing, energy and groceries. Economists and markets are now split between a hold and a later increase if price pressure returns. The next major test will be the RBA's August decision and the next round of labour and inflation data.
