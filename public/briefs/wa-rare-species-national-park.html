Rare species discovery puts a WA national park in focus

A rare species has been identified in a Western Australian national park, adding to concerns about how small habitats are protected. The discovery matters because new finds can change conservation priorities and influence land management. The next step is to confirm how widespread the species is and whether it needs extra protection.
