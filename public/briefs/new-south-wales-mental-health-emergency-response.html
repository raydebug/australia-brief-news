NSW expands clinician-led responses to mental-health crises

New South Wales is rolling out a Virtual Mental Health Hub and specialist response teams so more Triple Zero mental-health calls are handled by health workers instead of police. The program is funded at $270 million over 10 years and was pushed by repeated cases where families said police-led responses made crises worse. It matters because first contact can shape whether someone gets care, calms down or escalates. The next test is how quickly the model expands statewide and whether it improves safety and response times.
