Economists still expect the RBA to hold rates

Economists and lenders are still expecting the Reserve Bank to keep the cash rate unchanged at next week's meeting. The debate is not settled, though, because inflation, wage growth and household spending are still pulling in different directions. This matters for borrowers who are watching for any sign of another move on mortgage costs. The key uncertainty is whether the bank keeps waiting or surprises the market with a hike.
