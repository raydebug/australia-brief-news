News Corp's earnings sharpen the fight over AI scraping in Australia

News Corp says its Australian mastheads kept almost one million subscribers, but the company's wider news division again faced weaker digital revenue and falling interest in print. Chief executive Robert Thomson used the results to attack AI firms and content buyers he says profit from scraped journalism without permission. The story matters because it ties media revenue, copyright and AI regulation together for Australian publishers. The open issue is whether lawsuits, licensing deals and any new rules will slow the flow of content into AI systems.
