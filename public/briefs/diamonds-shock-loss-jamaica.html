Jamaica ends Australia's netball gold defence in a one-goal shock

Australia's Diamonds lost 46-45 to Jamaica in the Commonwealth Games semifinal after Sophie Dwyer's last shot missed on the siren. The defeat sends Australia into the bronze-medal match and marks its first semifinal loss at these Games. It matters because the team entered as defending champion and expected to reach the title game again, so the result resets the medal path for one of Australia's most watched teams. The next step is the bronze match and a hard review of how the final minutes slipped away.
