Origin's data breach exposes records of about 900,000 Australians

Origin Energy says a cyber incident may have exposed personal records for about 900,000 current and former customers. The leaked material is reported to include names, addresses, dates of birth, phone numbers and account details, which matters because it can feed scams and identity theft. The next step is the police investigation and the company's customer notifications.
