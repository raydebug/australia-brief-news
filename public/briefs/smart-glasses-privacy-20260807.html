Australia weighs tougher privacy rules as camera glasses spread

Cheap smart glasses with built-in cameras have put Australian privacy rules back under pressure. The attorney-general has asked the privacy commissioner to examine the issue after Kmart's $89 pair sold out and critics warned about covert recording. That matters because the devices are becoming cheaper and more common before the law has clear guardrails. The next step is whether Canberra treats this as a privacy update or leaves most of the policing to existing state and territory laws.
