Teen killed after being hit twice on Adelaide's Hindley Street

Police in Adelaide say an 18-year-old died after being struck by one car and then hit again by another on Hindley Street. The crash happened in the city centre and left emergency crews treating the teenager for life-threatening injuries before he was pronounced dead. It matters because it is another reminder of how quickly a traffic collision can turn fatal on a busy urban street. Investigators still need to work out the exact sequence, and dashcam or CCTV footage could help.
