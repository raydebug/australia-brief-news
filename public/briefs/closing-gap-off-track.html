Closing the Gap targets are still badly off pace

Australia has reached only one Closing the Gap target so far, with First Nations pre-school enrolment the lone national milestone already met. The new report says several other targets are improving but still off track, while incarceration, child safety and some health outcomes are moving the wrong way. That matters because the 2031 deadline is already halfway gone and governments still have to show real progress, not just new statements. The next step is whether federal and state governments turn the report into funded, measurable action with Aboriginal-led organisations.
