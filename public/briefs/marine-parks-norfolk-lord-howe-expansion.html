Australia moves to expand marine park protections around Norfolk and Lord Howe islands

The federal government has proposed expanding marine park protections around Norfolk Island and Lord Howe Island by about 670,000 square kilometres and lifting some areas to highly protected status. The plan would block fishing, seabed mining and other extractive activity in those waters, which matters because the islands support rare species and important tourism and coastal ecosystems. The next step is consultation, and that will show how much of the proposal survives pressure from fishing and regional interests.
