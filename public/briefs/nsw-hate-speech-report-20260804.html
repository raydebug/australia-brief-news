NSW rejects widening hate speech laws beyond race

New South Wales has decided not to extend its hate speech laws beyond racial vilification, despite a review that recommended broader protections. Religion, gender identity and sexual orientation were among the attributes left outside the change, so the legal limit stays where it was. The significance is that the state has drawn a line on how far the law should go, and campaigners on both sides are likely to keep pressing the issue.
