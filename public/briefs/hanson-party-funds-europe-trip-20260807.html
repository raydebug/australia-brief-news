Pauline Hanson faces scrutiny over party-funded Europe trip

Pauline Hanson has confirmed One Nation paid for luxury accommodation during her recent Europe trip. She says the party covered the hotel bill in London, while Gina Rinehart paid for some flights and other travel costs, after questions about whether the rooms were left unused. It matters because the dispute goes to party finances, donor influence and the standard voters expect from elected leaders. The next step is whether the pressure grows as more receipts and travel details are examined.
