Isaac Cooper steps down after Glasgow drinking breach

Australian swimmer Isaac Cooper has stepped down from the Dolphins' leadership group after reports he was drinking instead of supporting teammates during the Glasgow Commonwealth Games. Swimming Australia said he missed the final night of competition and a team meeting, which went against team expectations. The incident matters because Cooper is one of Australia's better-known swimmers and the team had relied on him in relay and backstroke events. He is still scheduled to race at the Pan Pacific Championships in the United States on August 12, so the next question is whether the sanction affects his longer-term place in the squad.
