Lady Elliot Island's damaged reef is bouncing back after Cyclone Alfred

At Lady Elliot Island, coral damaged by Cyclone Alfred has recovered far more than expected after cooler water temperatures eased reef stress. ABC says about 90 per cent of the affected coral has bounced back, and another spawning event could lift recovery further. The story matters because the reef has already been hit by repeated bleaching and weather shocks, so any strong recovery is important for Queensland's marine environment. The next question is whether that recovery lasts through the next warm spell and how much the coming spawn helps.
