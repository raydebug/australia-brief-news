Australia names an under-19 cricket squad for an India tour

Cricket Australia has named a 16-player under-19 squad for a multi-format tour of India from September 13 to October 9. The side will play three one-day matches and two red-ball games in Rajkot and Ahmedabad, with Tim Nielsen leading the program. That matters because India is a demanding place for young players to adapt, especially across both limited-overs and longer-form cricket. The main question now is how quickly this group settles into the conditions and whether the squad stays unchanged before departure.
