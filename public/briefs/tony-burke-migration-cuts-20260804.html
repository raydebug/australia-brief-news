Tony Burke delays migration cuts while Labor rewrites visa priorities

Tony Burke has pushed back the National Press Club announcement while Labor reshapes skilled visa processing to move some temporary applicants ahead in the queue. The move is meant to slow net overseas migration, and it comes amid pressure from the Coalition and One Nation over migration levels. The policy details still matter because they affect who can stay, who waits longer, and how quickly the government can reduce arrivals.
