Omar Musa wins the Miles Franklin for Fierceland

Omar Musa has won the 2026 Miles Franklin Literary Award for Fierceland, taking home the $60,000 prize for a novel built around family ties, inheritance and the damage done to Borneo's rainforests. The result matters because the award remains Australia's top fiction prize and again puts Southeast Asian-Australian storytelling at the centre of the national literary conversation. Musa said the book grew from years of work and personal connections to the region, while the judges singled out its mix of rhythm, politics and ecological loss. The next question is how widely the book reaches readers beyond the prize announcement.
