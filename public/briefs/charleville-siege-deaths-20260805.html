Charleville siege ends with two deaths and a coroner's investigation

Police in outback Queensland ended an eight-hour siege in Charleville to find a 45-year-old man and a 23-year-old pregnant woman dead inside the home. The scene will now be examined by the coroner and by police internal investigators. It matters because a serious assault call escalated into a major emergency that locked down several streets in a remote town. The immediate task is to piece together how the standoff unfolded and what officers found when they went in.
