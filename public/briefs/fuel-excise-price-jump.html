Motorists brace for a higher pump price as fuel relief expires

Australia's temporary fuel excise relief is ending, and petrol and diesel prices are expected to edge higher as retailers work through the tax change. The cut had already been stepped down, so the final change is less about a single shock than a slow pass-through at the bowser. It matters because fuel costs feed into household budgets, freight prices and the inflation outlook. The next thing to watch is how quickly the increase shows up and whether any fresh support is discussed if oil markets move again.
