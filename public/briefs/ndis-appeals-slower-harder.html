NDIS appeals could become slower and harder as the scheme tightens

Advocates say people challenging NDIS decisions already face a difficult process, and the federal government's tightening of the scheme could make reviews even slower. The worry is that vulnerable Australians may wait longer for tribunal outcomes that affect support, funding and everyday care. That matters because for many families the appeal system is the last chance to correct a decision that can reshape daily life. The next test is whether Canberra gives the tribunal more capacity to handle the load.
