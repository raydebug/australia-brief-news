Australia may tax big tech if publisher deals stall

Australia is moving toward a tougher news bargaining plan that would put more pressure on large digital platforms to strike deals with local publishers. The policy matters because it is meant to keep journalism funded when platforms use Australian news to hold readers and advertising. The next step is the final shape of the incentive and whether smaller outlets benefit from it.
