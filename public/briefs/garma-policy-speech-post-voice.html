Garma speech sharpens debate over post-Voice Indigenous policy

Julian Leeser used a Garma Festival speech in Arnhem Land to argue that the federal government has lost direction on Indigenous affairs since the Voice referendum. SBS also framed the address as a broader Coalition policy pitch, with education, a review of the Indigenous affairs agency and suicide prevention in focus. The speech matters because Garma remains one of the main national stages for Indigenous policy debate. What happens next will depend on whether the Coalition turns the speech into a clearer platform and whether the government responds with its own reset.
