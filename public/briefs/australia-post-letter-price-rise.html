Australia Post gets approval for a September stamp rise

Australia Post will lift the basic stamp price to $1.85 from 1 September after the regulator declined to block the increase. The company says letter volumes have fallen to levels not seen since the late 1930s, which is why the reserved mail network is losing money even after earlier price changes. The impact on households should be modest, but businesses that still rely on letters will feel it more, and the Communications Minister could still intervene before the change starts.
