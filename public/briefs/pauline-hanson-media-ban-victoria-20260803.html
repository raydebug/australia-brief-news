Pauline Hanson's media ban sparks a free-press row

Pauline Hanson barred the ABC, The Guardian and The Age from a Victorian press event, then defended the move as a temporary message to journalists she says have been unfair. The dispute came as One Nation was unveiling candidates and trying to build momentum in state politics. It matters because it raises a basic question about access, scrutiny and whether elected leaders can decide who gets into the room. Hanson later said the ban would not last forever, but she has not backed away from the criticism.
