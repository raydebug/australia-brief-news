Australia is preparing tighter rules for men's behaviour-change programs

Australia is moving toward tighter standards for men's behaviour-change programs, from short school sessions to longer courses for men using violence at home. The change matters because these programs sit inside the broader domestic violence response and are often used by schools, courts and community services. The open question is how strict the standards will be and how quickly providers can adjust. What happens next will decide whether the rules improve consistency or create another layer of paperwork.
