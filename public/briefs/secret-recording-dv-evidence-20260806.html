High Court ruling could widen the use of secret recordings in abuse cases

The High Court has ruled that a secret recording can be used as evidence in a domestic violence case when it protects the victim's safety and autonomy. The decision involved a woman who recorded her husband's admission after earlier abuse allegations. It matters because the ruling gives survivors a new tool in some cases, but courts will still have to decide how far the exception goes. Future cases will test the limits.
