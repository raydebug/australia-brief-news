Queensland Labor MP renews push for a greyhound racing ban

Ipswich MP Jennifer Howard used a rally at Brisbane's Queens Park to press Labor to back a ban on greyhound racing in Queensland. The debate matters because it now touches animal welfare, the future of the $90 million The Q facility in Ipswich and the political split over whether the sport should continue. The next question is whether Labor turns the rally into policy or leaves the sport in place while the controversy keeps growing.
