Tasmania's greyhound phase-out is moving closer

Tasmania's bid to end greyhound racing is edging forward, with the government still trying to secure the support it needs in the upper house. Officials are also offering a voluntary exit package for participants who leave the industry this year. The issue matters because it affects animal welfare, racing jobs and the way Tasmania handles a long political fight over the sport.
