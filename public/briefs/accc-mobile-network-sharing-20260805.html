ACCC inquiry could force mobile networks to share coverage in regional Australia

The ACCC has launched an inquiry into whether mobile customers should be able to roam onto another network when their own provider has no signal. The review will look at domestic roaming and possible infrastructure sharing, with a focus on regional and rural Australia. It matters because recent outages at Telstra and Optus exposed how fragile coverage can be when one network fails. The key question is whether the watchdog can improve reliability without weakening investment in new towers.
