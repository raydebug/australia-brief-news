Blundy reclaims Bonds and Sheridan in a $700m retail deal

Brett Blundy's investment group has agreed to buy Bonds, Sheridan, Berlei and Bras N Things from Gildan in a $700 million deal. Once the transaction closes, the brands are expected to return to Australian ownership and sit under one local owner again. It matters because several familiar labels are being folded back together in a tight retail market, and the price shows how much value remains in Australian consumer brands. The next step is regulatory approval and completion, with closing expected in the second half of 2026.
