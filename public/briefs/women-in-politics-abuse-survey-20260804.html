Abuse of women in politics is worsening, survey warns

A new survey says most Australian women in public office or running for election have faced online abuse tied to their political role. The findings suggest the problem is not isolated noise but a barrier to participation, especially when abuse is amplified by deepfakes and other AI tools. That matters because it can push capable people out of politics and narrow who feels safe standing for office. The next question is what protections and platform rules can actually reduce the abuse.
