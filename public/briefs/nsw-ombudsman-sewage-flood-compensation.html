NSW tenant should be fully compensated after repeated sewage floods, ombudsman says

A New South Wales public housing tenant who lived through repeated sewage overflows is now set for compensation after an ombudsman found Homes NSW handled the case poorly. The findings say her belongings were left contaminated and then moved without proper cleaning or return, turning a maintenance failure into a wider loss for a vulnerable renter. It matters because public housing agencies are supposed to respond quickly and accountably when homes become unsafe. Homes NSW has accepted the recommendations, but the question now is how fast compensation and process changes actually follow.
