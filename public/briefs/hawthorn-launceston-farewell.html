Hawthorn's Launceston farewell keeps its Tasmania story alive for one more year

Hawthorn closed out another Launceston outing with a win over North Melbourne, leaving the club still in the finals mix. Nick Watson kicked five goals and Mabior Chol provided the late buffer, while the game also marked one more chapter in Hawthorn's long Tasmania connection. It matters because the club's relationship with Launceston has shaped its identity for decades and is now approaching a reset. The next focus is the run home and how the club adjusts once that link changes after next season.
