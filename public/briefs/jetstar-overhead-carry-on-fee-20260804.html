Jetstar turns overhead carry-on into a paid extra

Jetstar will change its cabin-bag rules from February 2027, leaving one small underseat bag free while a larger bag for the overhead locker moves into a paid Priority Carry-on tier. Reported prices range from about $25 on shorter routes to more than $50 on longer ones, and the old 7 kilogram total allowance will be dropped. The shift matters because it changes how budget fares are sold and how passengers plan for short-haul travel. The key question now is whether travellers accept the new trade-off or start looking for rivals that keep overhead space bundled in.
