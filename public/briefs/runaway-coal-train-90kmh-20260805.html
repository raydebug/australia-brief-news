A runaway coal train is reported to have hit 90 kilometres an hour

An investigation into a runaway coal train says the train reached about 90 kilometres an hour over a 20-kilometre stretch. The report brings safety systems, braking procedures and rail oversight back into focus after a prolonged loss of control. Authorities still need to decide whether any rules or safeguards should change.
