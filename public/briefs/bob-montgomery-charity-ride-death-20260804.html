Bob Montgomery dies after finishing his cross-country MND ride

Bob Montgomery has died a day after completing a 5,300-kilometre ride across Australia to raise awareness and funds for motor neurone disease. The journey mattered because it turned a family campaign into a national story and drew attention to the support services people with MND need. The immediate question is how the fundraising total will be used and how his family and supporters choose to mark the ride and the man behind it.
