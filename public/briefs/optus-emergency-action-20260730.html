Optus faces court action over the outage tied to emergency call failures

The telecoms regulator has started legal action against Optus over the 2025 outage linked to missed emergency calls and deaths. The case goes to whether the company handled the failure fast enough and met its obligations to protect triple zero service. It matters because emergency communications only work if the network is reliable when people need it most. The court outcome could reshape penalties and the pressure on telcos to harden their systems.
