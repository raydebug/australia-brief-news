Sydney hospitals tell non-urgent patients to stay away

Sydney hospitals are under heavy winter pressure after emergency departments logged a record day of demand and authorities urged people to stay away unless the situation is urgent. The strain is worst in western and south-western Sydney, where long waits, ambulance demand and bed shortages are all feeding into the backlog. It matters because it shows how quickly hospital stress can spread across the system, and the government is now trying to steer non-urgent patients toward urgent care and advice lines.
