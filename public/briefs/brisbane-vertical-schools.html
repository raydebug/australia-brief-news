Brisbane's vertical schools are changing the inner-city classroom

Vertical schools have become a feature of Brisbane's inner city, using a much smaller footprint while still giving students room to learn and socialise. The model matters because it is one response to density, housing pressure and the need to build schools where land is scarce. The open question is whether the format can keep working as enrolments and city development continue to change.
