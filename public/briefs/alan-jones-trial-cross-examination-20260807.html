Alan Jones trial moves into deeper cross-examination

Alan Jones is still on trial in Sydney after pleading not guilty to 22 counts of indecent assault and sexual touching involving six complainants. The latest hearing focused on the first complainant's cross-examination, with courtroom evidence still being tested and some publication limits briefly in place. It matters because the case is one of the country's highest-profile sexual-assault trials involving a public figure. The next step is more evidence, more witnesses and more rulings on what can be reported.
