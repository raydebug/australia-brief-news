Blundy buys back Bonds and sister brands for $700 million

Brett Blundy has bought Bras N Things, Bonds, Berlei and Sheridan back into Australian ownership in a $700 million deal. The brands were sold by Hanesbrands after a review of its local business, and the purchase puts a group of familiar retail names back under one Australian investor. It matters because the deal shifts control of some of the country's best-known clothing and home labels. The next question is how Blundy uses the portfolio across stores, supply chains and future expansion.
