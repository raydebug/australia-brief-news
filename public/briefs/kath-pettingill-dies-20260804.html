Kath Pettingill dies, closing a notorious Melbourne crime chapter

Kath Pettingill has died, ending another long chapter in the public story of one of Melbourne's most notorious crime families. She was the mother of the Walsh Street police killers, and her name stayed tied to the violence and notoriety of that era. The significance is less about a new investigation than about the end of a figure who became part of Victoria's criminal history. The next reaction will be how family, media and the public frame that legacy.
