Queensland border force intercepts $2 million in fake notes

The Australian Border Force says it has seized more than $2 million in counterfeit currency in Queensland, the state's biggest fake-cash bust linked to a single importer. The haul matters because fake notes are getting harder to spot and more of them are entering the country through air cargo and mail. Authorities say the consignments came from Asia and were intercepted before they reached circulation. The next step is forensic work and whether investigators identify broader supply chains.
