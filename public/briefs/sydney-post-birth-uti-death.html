A Sydney coroner says a preventable post-birth infection killed Gia Lam

A coronial finding in Sydney says Gia Lam's death three days after giving birth was preventable after a urinary tract infection was missed. The case matters because it shows how a common infection can become fatal if warning signs are not caught and explained clearly after childbirth. Lam's family is now speaking publicly, and the findings put pressure on hospitals to improve postpartum checks, interpreter access and follow-up. The next step is whether the inquest leads to practical changes in maternity care.
