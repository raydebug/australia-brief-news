Fuel prices push EVs and hybrids to a record share of Australian car sales

Australian car buyers kept moving toward electric and hybrid vehicles as the latest market data showed almost half of all sales from April to June had some form of electrified powertrain. Battery-electric and plug-in hybrid sales both hit fresh records, while the share of pure petrol cars kept shrinking. The fuel excise cut has now ended, so pump prices are likely to rise again. The key question is whether this shift in buying habits holds once the immediate price pressure eases.
