Gold Coast builder fined after polluted water leaked into canals

Gold Coast council investigators say a builder's dewatering system allowed contaminated water to escape into Palm Beach canals. The pollution left the water rust-coloured and led to a fine after the site was checked. It matters because construction runoff can spread quickly through sensitive coastal waterways when controls fail. The next issue is whether tougher monitoring will stop a repeat.
