Cheap camera glasses trigger privacy backlash across Australia

Kmart's $89 camera glasses have sold out across Australia, turning a low-cost recording device into a mainstream retail product. The glasses can capture photos and high-definition video, answer calls and run an AI assistant, which is why privacy advocates say they create a real risk of people being filmed without consent. The next question is whether regulators and lawmakers act on the public backlash or let the technology spread under the current rules.
