Sydney protest evidence broadens the antisemitism inquiry

The antisemitism royal commission in Sydney is now weighing testimony about controversial protests, including a pro-Palestinian rally and a neo-Nazi demonstration. The hearing matters because it broadens the inquiry from individual incidents to the way protests are policed and how hate spreads into public life. The next step is more evidence from witnesses and law enforcement on what happened and what should change.
