Barnaby Joyce's refugee stance is back under scrutiny

Barnaby Joyce's view on refugees is back in focus after a Guardian podcast revisited the gap between his support for resettlement in Armidale and One Nation's harder migration line. The discussion matters because it shows how regional needs and national party policy can pull in opposite directions on migration. The next question is whether Joyce changes how he talks about refugees or simply keeps the contradiction in place.
