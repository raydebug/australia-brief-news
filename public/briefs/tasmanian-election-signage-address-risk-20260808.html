Tasmanian candidates warn address rules on campaign signs could put families at risk

Independent candidates in Tasmania say rules that force them to print a home address on campaign material can expose them and their families. The complaint matters because personal safety is now colliding with election disclosure rules that were written long before modern online harassment. The next step is whether lawmakers change the law before local elections, or leave candidates to keep using home addresses.
