Australia's eSafety regulator moves against Telegram

Australia's eSafety regulator has launched court action that could end with a $54.6 million penalty against Telegram over alleged failures to remove pro-terror material. Regulators say the case involves violent propaganda and extremist content that stayed accessible after reports from users. It matters because it tests how Australian online-safety law applies to a global messaging platform with a large local audience. The court case will decide whether Telegram breached its duties and what comes next.
