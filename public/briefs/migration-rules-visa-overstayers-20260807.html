Cabinet weighs tougher rules for visa overstayers

The federal government is considering changes that would strip work rights from visa overstayers whose protection claims are rejected. The broader package could also touch working-holiday, family and appeal settings, but cabinet has not locked down the details. It matters because the plan affects border enforcement, labour supply and family reunion policy at the same time. The next step is whether the proposal is finalised and how far it goes after internal debate.
