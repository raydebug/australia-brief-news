Labor's gambling overhaul is running into Senate resistance

A federal gambling reform package is facing resistance in the Senate as the Coalition, Greens and several independents press for tougher limits on ads and inducements. The debate sits alongside inquiry evidence about betting firms allegedly supplying drugs and sex workers to high-value clients, which the companies deny. The issue matters because Labor wants to show it can tighten gambling rules without losing control of the wider bill. The next step is whether ministers amend the package enough to secure support before the inquiry reports on August 17.
