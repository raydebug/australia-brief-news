Victorian farmer offers reward after family dog is shot at nursery

A farmer in regional Victoria has offered a $20,000 reward after someone shot the family dog at a busy nursery. The case matters because it has rattled a small community and raised fears about a violent drive-by attack in a place people expect to be safe. Police now need a lead, and the main unanswered question is who fired the shot and why.
