David Brat gets the final nod for the US posting in Canberra

The US Senate has confirmed David Brat as the next ambassador to Australia after the post sat open for months. The appointment matters because Washington and Canberra are still dealing with tariffs, defence coordination and AUKUS pressure. Brat says he wants to focus on trade, critical minerals and security ties, and the next step is his arrival in Canberra.
