Maternity-leave side project grows into a community pantry in NSW

Two mothers in northern New South Wales turned spare fruit and vegetables into a community pantry that now helps local families. What began as a maternity-leave side project has become a small but practical response to food pressure in the town of Guyra. It matters because it shows how local volunteer ideas can fill gaps when cost-of-living stress hits a regional community, and the question now is how long the effort can stay sustainable.
