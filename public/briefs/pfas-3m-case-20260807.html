Australia's PFAS case against 3M goes to court

The Commonwealth has opened its PFAS case against 3M, arguing the company's firefighting foam contaminated defence sites and left the public with a huge cleanup bill. The claim is for billions of dollars and could become one of the government's biggest environmental actions. It matters because the case will test how far corporate responsibility reaches for so-called forever chemicals. The next step is the court's assessment of the evidence and liability.
