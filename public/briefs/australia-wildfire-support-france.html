Australia sends crews to help France battle record wildfires

Australia is sending aircraft and specialist crews to help France as extreme wildfires continue across parts of France and Spain. The deployment matters because it shows how quickly the southern summer fire season experience in Australia is being shared abroad during an unusually severe European fire run. The next question is whether the extra support helps contain the blazes before hotter conditions return.
