Schools are being warned about AI misuse of student photos

Australian schools are being urged to be more careful about the photos they post online after warnings that images can be copied and altered with AI. The concern matters because school portraits and staff photos can be turned into harmful deepfake material. The next step is whether schools tighten image-sharing rules and eSafety guidance keeps spreading.
