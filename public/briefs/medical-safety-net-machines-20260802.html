Medical safety net strain leaves hospitals juggling thousands of machines

A new report says the medical safety net is under strain, with thousands of machines and only a small team available to keep them working. That raises the risk of delays, breakdowns and missed care when equipment fails. The pressure now is on hospitals and governments to fix the staffing and maintenance gap before more problems pile up.
