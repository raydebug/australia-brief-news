NSW rejects wider hate speech protections

The Minns government has ruled out extending NSW hate speech laws to cover more groups after a review it commissioned. The report recommended broader protection, but ministers said the legal and practical questions need more work before any change can land. The issue matters because it sits at the point where free speech, anti-discrimination law and community safety collide. The next step is whether the government revisits the recommendation or leaves the law as it is.
