Fuel prices are set to climb as the excise cut ends

Drivers are facing a new rise at the pump as the temporary fuel excise cut ends, with wholesale petrol set to move up 16 cents a litre and diesel costs also expected to rise. ABC says the change took effect from midnight and that the higher costs will filter through over the next 10 days, while AAP and The Guardian both put pump prices around $2 a litre or higher. The story matters because households, regional drivers and truck operators were already under pressure from higher oil prices and renewed tension in the Middle East. The open question is how fast retailers pass on the increase and whether fuel markets stay volatile.

Source differences

  • ABC focuses on when the change reaches bowsers and the impact on diesel users.
  • AAP emphasises the government line and the end of the heavy-vehicle charge discount.
