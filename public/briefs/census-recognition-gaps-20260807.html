Pressure grows to fix the census's blind spots

Advocates say the 2026 Census still risks missing people whose living arrangements or identities do not fit the standard form. If those groups are undercounted, funding and service planning can drift away from real need. That matters for housing, health and community programs that rely on census data. The key issue now is whether the ABS makes any changes before the count is locked in and the results start guiding decisions.
