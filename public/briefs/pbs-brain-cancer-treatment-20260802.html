PBS listing brings the first new brain-cancer treatment in two decades

A new drug for a rare brain cancer has been added to the PBS, giving Australians access to the first treatment of its kind in about 20 years. The change matters because the disease is hard to treat and the cost of private access can be a major barrier. Doctors and families will now watch how widely the medicine is taken up and whether it changes outcomes for patients living with the cancer.
