Ben Carroll starts a Victoria cabinet reset after Allan steps aside

Ben Carroll is set to unveil a new Victorian ministry on Monday after replacing Jacinta Allan. The reshuffle follows Allan's resignation and the departure of two senior ministers, leaving vacancies that will be filled before the November state election. It matters because the front bench will shape Labor's response to construction corruption claims, school bargaining and the push to show a clean break from the old leadership. The immediate unknown is how Carroll balances faction demands, cabinet numbers and policy pressure while trying to steady the government.
