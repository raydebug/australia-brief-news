South Australian officer leaves hospital after alleged stabbing

A South Australian police officer has left hospital after an alleged stabbing in Adelaide's north. Police say the suspect was shot in the leg during the response and now faces a serious criminal investigation. The case matters because it shows how quickly frontline policing can turn violent, and because investigators still need to test the evidence, witness accounts and court process.
