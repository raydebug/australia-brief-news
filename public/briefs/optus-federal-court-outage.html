ACMA takes Optus to court over the 2025 triple-zero outage

Australia's communications regulator has taken Optus to Federal Court over the 2025 triple-zero outage that left emergency calls unable to connect. The case says the failure breached emergency-call obligations across multiple jurisdictions and could leave the telco facing very large penalties. It also keeps pressure on the industry to prove that network upgrades and other technical changes cannot put triple zero at risk. The next step is Optus's defence and the court timetable.
