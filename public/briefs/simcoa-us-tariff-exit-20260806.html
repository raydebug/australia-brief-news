Simcoa says it will pull out of the US after a fresh tariff hit

Australia's only silicon producer says it will leave the US market next week after American trade authorities imposed an extra 40 per cent tariff on its exports. Simcoa says the ruling makes the business untenable and leaves it looking for other markets in South-East Asia and India. It matters because the case shows how US trade action can quickly reshape Australian critical-minerals exports. The next question is whether Canberra pushes back or accepts the move as a commercial decision.
