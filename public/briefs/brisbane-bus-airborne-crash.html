Airborne car slams into Brisbane bus in an Archerfield crash

A Toyota Yaris was launched into the air in Archerfield and smashed into the side of a Brisbane bus. The bus was empty, but the impact left the vehicle badly damaged and sent the driver of the car to hospital in shock. Police say the crash began with a chain of lane changes, and investigators are still piecing together exactly how it unfolded.
