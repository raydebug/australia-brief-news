Melbourne's deputy lord mayor is set for a state politics move

Roshena Campbell is set to take the Liberal Party's top spot for Victoria's Western Metropolitan Region, moving her from Melbourne Town Hall toward state parliament. If confirmed, the change would create a vacancy in the deputy lord mayor role and reshuffle the city's leadership. It matters because it could alter both the Liberal ticket and Melbourne council's balance. The next step is formal endorsement and the council response to the vacancy.
