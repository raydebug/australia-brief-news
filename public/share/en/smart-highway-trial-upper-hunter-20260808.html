NSW trials a smart speed system on the Golden Highway to protect local traffic

New South Wales has started a 12-month trial of smart roadside speed signs on the Golden Highway in the Upper Hunter. The system uses vehicle detection to slow traffic when drivers are turning off side roads, which matters because heavy vehicle volumes are rising around renewable energy projects. The aim is to cut crash risk without putting blanket limits on the whole corridor. The next step is whether the trial delivers safer turns and fewer serious incidents.
