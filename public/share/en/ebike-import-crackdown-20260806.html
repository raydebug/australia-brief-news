The federal government moves to tighten rules on dangerous e-bike imports

The government will ask the ACCC to develop a national safety standard for e-bikes, e-scooters and e-skateboards sold in Australia. The plan would cap power and speed, with the proposed standard aiming to stop high-powered devices linked to injuries and deaths. It matters because the bikes are already common on roads and footpaths, and state rules have not been enough to stop dangerous models spreading. The next step is how quickly the standard can be written and enforced across states and territories.
