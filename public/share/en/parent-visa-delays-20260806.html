Australia's parent visa delays keep families waiting for years

Australia's parent visa system is under renewed pressure after reporting showed more than 1,500 applicants died during a 21-month stretch while waiting. The contributory parent visa costs about $50,000 and can still take around 15 years, while cheaper parent visas can take far longer. The issue matters because it leaves migrant families stuck between high fees, long queues and ageing relatives overseas. The next question is whether the government folds parent visas into its wider migration changes or leaves the backlog untouched.
