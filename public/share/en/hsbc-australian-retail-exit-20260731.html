HSBC begins its Australian retail exit

HSBC will wind down its Australian retail bank over 18 months after selling a $36 billion home and personal loan book to Blackstone. About 19 branches are set to close while the lender keeps its corporate, institutional, asset management and private banking arms. The move matters because it further shrinks face-to-face banking choices for customers and staff across Australia. The sale still needs approval, and the next steps will focus on how loans, accounts and any job changes are handled.
