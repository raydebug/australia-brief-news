Australia sets up a national e-bike safety crackdown

The federal government has asked the ACCC to draft national standards for electric bikes, scooters and skateboards after injuries kept rising and state rules stayed patchy. The move is meant to close loopholes that let retailers sell high-powered devices that do not meet a consistent benchmark. It matters because e-bike use has expanded faster than the rules built to control it. The standards now move into consultation before they can be enforced.
