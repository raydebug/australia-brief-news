Sydney driver arrested after bus-stop hit-and-run injures a teen

Police say an 18-year-old was hurt after an Audi struck a bus stop in Sydney's north shore and the driver was later arrested. The case matters because a busy roadside stop turned into a violent crash scene, leaving an innocent teenager in hospital. Investigators now have to pin down the driver's movements before impact and whether more charges will follow.
