NAPLAN scores still show too many students missing basics

Australia's latest NAPLAN release shows reading, writing and numeracy pressure is still widespread, with roughly one in three students below the expected standard in at least some areas. The results matter because they shape how schools, states and federal ministers target tutoring, curriculum changes and extra support. The picture is mixed: numeracy has improved in some year levels, while writing and reading remain weaker and gaps persist between city, regional and disadvantaged students. The next test is whether the new data leads to classroom changes that reach the students falling furthest behind.
