NSW puts clinicians first in more crisis callouts after Bondi

New South Wales is shifting more mental-health crisis calls toward clinicians, with a health-led triage model taking a bigger role. The change follows the Bondi Junction inquiry and lines up with plans for a virtual hub inside NSW Ambulance, while police still attend when safety risks are high. It matters because it aims to lower escalation at first contact and get distressed people to medical care faster. The key test is whether the rollout is quick, workable and staffed properly.
