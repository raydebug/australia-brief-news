NT Aboriginal police officers launch racial discrimination class action

Current and former Aboriginal community police officers in the Northern Territory have filed a Federal Court class action, saying they were exposed to decades of racial discrimination, vilification and unfair pay inside NT Police. They say the culture also limited promotion chances and left them facing offensive language and unequal treatment compared with non-Indigenous colleagues. The case matters because it turns long-running complaints into a legal test of whether reform has actually changed the force. The first hearing is set for August 19, and the proceedings could still run into 2027.
