Australia's 2026 census moves to the front of the national agenda

Households are being reminded that the 2026 census is due on 11 August, with most people in Australia required to take part. The form includes new questions on sex recorded at birth, gender and sexual orientation, and the penalties for ignoring the count or giving false answers remain steep. That matters because the census shapes funding, services and planning for years after the count is finished. The next watchpoint is whether the public debate around religion and privacy affects response quality.
