Sunshine shop owner's death leads to more charges and bail rulings

Van Viet Truong, the 60-year-old Sunshine shop owner, died after the alleged assault that began during a robbery attempt. Police have since pushed the case forward with more charges and bail decisions tied to the same incident. It matters because the case has become a test of how the justice system handles serious youth violence. The next step is further court action and any changes to the charges.
