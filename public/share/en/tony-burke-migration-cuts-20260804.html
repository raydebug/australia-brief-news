Tony Burke delays a speech that could spell out deeper migration cuts

Tony Burke has delayed a planned speech that was expected to spell out tougher migration settings. The timing matters because migration, housing and student visas are all under pressure inside Labor, and the delay suggests the government is still working through the politics before it goes public. The next thing to watch is when Burke reschedules the speech and how far any cutbacks go.
