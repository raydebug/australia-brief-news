High Court restores Emma Lovell killer's tougher sentence

The High Court has put back the tougher sentence for the man who killed Brisbane mother Emma Lovell in a 2022 home invasion. The court said the earlier reduction to a 60 per cent non-parole period should not have stood, so the original 14-year term remains the benchmark. That matters because the case has become a test of how far appeal courts can go when they revisit youth sentencing. The next step is the formal ripple through release timing and any broader guidance for similar appeals.
