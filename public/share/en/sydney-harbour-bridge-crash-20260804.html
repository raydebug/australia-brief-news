Three people are hurt after a crash slows Sydney Harbour Bridge traffic

A two-car crash on the Sydney Harbour Bridge injured three people and briefly disrupted one of the city's busiest road links. Even a small collision there can ripple through commuter traffic, freight and emergency access. The broader issue is that congestion and driver error can quickly turn a routine incident into a citywide delay. Police and traffic crews now have to sort out what caused the crash and how long repairs will take.
