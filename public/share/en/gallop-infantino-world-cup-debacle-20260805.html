David Gallop says Infantino's World Cup idea flunks the pub test

Former Football Australia boss David Gallop has sharply criticised Gianni Infantino's proposed FIFA commercial rights shake-up, saying it has landed badly with fans and administrators. The dispute matters because it goes to who controls football's biggest tournaments and how much commercial power FIFA should hand away. Gallop's comments add pressure on Infantino after the plan drew strong backlash, and the next step is whether FIFA backs away or tries to relaunch the idea in another form.
