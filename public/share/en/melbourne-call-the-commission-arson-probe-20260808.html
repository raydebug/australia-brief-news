Melbourne police charge teen in Commission-linked arson probe

Police in Melbourne have charged a 17-year-old as they widen an investigation into notes telling shop owners to 'call the Commission' before a run of arson attacks. Investigators say they have linked 19 notes, seized phones and SIM cards, and believe some young people have been drawn in by an offshore syndicate. The case matters because it points to a broader extortion network, not just isolated fires. The next step is the children's court process and any charges against higher-level organisers.
