Canberra tightens the rules for AI data centres

The federal government is moving to set firmer rules around how new AI data centres use power. The plan would require operators to prove they are adding enough electricity back into the grid and would rule out projects that rely entirely on gas. That matters because the data-centre boom is now colliding with energy capacity, state approvals and pressure on land use. The next step is whether Canberra can lock in the framework without driving investment away.
