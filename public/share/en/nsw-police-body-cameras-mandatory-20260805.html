NSW police now have to switch on body cameras when using powers

Thousands of NSW Police officers must now turn on body-worn cameras whenever they exercise police powers in an operational role. The change follows a Four Corners investigation and years of pressure from the police watchdog over cases where cameras were not used or were activated too late. The policy matters because it changes the evidence trail for arrests, pursuits and force used on the street. If an officer does not record, they have to explain why in writing, and the next test is how consistently the rule is enforced.
