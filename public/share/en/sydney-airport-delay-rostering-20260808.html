Sydney Airport delays ripple through thousands of passengers

Dozens of Sydney Airport flights were delayed after Airservices Australia said roster issues were affecting traffic management. More than 150 flights were caught up in the disruption, which can quickly spread through check-ins, connections and cancellations. The significance is not just the delay itself but what it says about how fragile the system can be when staffing slips. Passengers are still being told to check with airlines before heading to the airport.
