Bird flu spread in southern Australia triggers a wider wildlife response

H5 bird flu is spreading through southern Australia, with more confirmed and suspected cases among wild birds and growing concern for seals and other wildlife. Officials now count 175 confirmed or presumed positive detections, and local events such as the Royal Adelaide Show have already dropped bird exhibits. That matters because the outbreak is moving beyond isolated detections and forcing tougher biosecurity, wildlife monitoring and event changes. The next step is whether authorities can slow the virus before more colonies and coastal species are affected.
