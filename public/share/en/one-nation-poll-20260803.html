One Nation's poll surge keeps pressure on the Coalition

Fresh polling has put One Nation back near the front of the federal race, while Coalition figures again talk openly about whether they should work with Pauline Hanson's party. The result matters because it shows how much voter frustration is still reshaping the contest, especially on cost of living and migration. The next question is whether the numbers turn into a real preference deal or simply more pressure on the major parties.
