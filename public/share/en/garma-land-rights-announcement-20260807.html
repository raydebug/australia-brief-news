A Garma announcement points to a new chapter in Northern Territory land rights

At Garma, an announcement tied to a new land council is being framed against five decades of Northern Territory land-rights history. That makes the story bigger than a festival moment, because it goes to who represents communities and how future land decisions are made. It matters for governance, recognition and local control. The next step is whether the proposal becomes a formal body with real powers or remains a symbolic signal.
