Angus Taylor is drawn back into the NSW ICAC Reformers inquiry

Text messages and hearing evidence are bringing Angus Taylor back into the NSW ICAC inquiry into the Reformers faction. Witnesses are being asked about fundraising, private payments and whether Taylor was ever approached for money, while he denies any improper donation. It matters because the probe now reaches from a state corruption hearing into federal opposition politics and internal party fundraising rules. The next step is more witness evidence and whether investigators turn any of the allegations into formal findings.
