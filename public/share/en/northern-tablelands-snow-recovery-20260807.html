Northern Tablelands landowners are still rebuilding after record snow

A year after the Northern Tablelands was hit by the deepest snow in living memory, some landowners are still repairing fences, roads and pasture damage. The clean-up has stretched far beyond the storm itself because the weight of the snow damaged basic farm infrastructure and slowed recovery. The story matters because it shows how a rare weather event can keep hurting rural communities long after the headlines fade. The next question is whether stronger disaster support and better preparation can shorten the recovery the next time extreme snow hits.
