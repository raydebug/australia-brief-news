Australia's 2026 Census faces privacy scrutiny and missing-paper complaints

The 2026 Census is due on 11 August, and ABS officials are telling households to complete the form online or on paper. Reports that some people have not yet received instructions have added to privacy questions around the new gender, sexuality and ancestry items. It matters because census data drives funding and planning for services across the country. The key risk now is whether the ABS can get clear instructions out in time and avoid a scramble on census night.
