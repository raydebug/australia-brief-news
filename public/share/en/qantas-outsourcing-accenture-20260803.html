Qantas weighs a major back-office shift with Accenture

Qantas is in talks with Accenture over a large outsourcing move that could shift about 1,000 back-office jobs offshore. The issue matters because it sits between cost cutting, automation and the future of Australian jobs at the national carrier. The next step is whether the talks become a formal plan and how workers and unions respond.
