Remote Indigenous designers win Darwin fashion awards

Artists from remote communities have won major prizes at this year's National Indigenous Fashion Awards in Darwin. The event puts First Nations designers, makers and models in front of a national audience and highlights work from remote parts of the country as well as city studios. It matters because the awards can turn cultural knowledge into income, recognition and ongoing commissions. The next question is whether that attention leads to support that lasts beyond one night.
