Older women are slipping deeper into the housing crisis

New reporting from Queensland shows more older women turning to support services as rents rise and savings run thin. Many are living alone, have little housing security, or are trying to recover after a relationship breakdown, so even a short delay can push them toward homelessness. The policy test is whether governments add crisis accommodation, rental help and longer-term housing supply before the backlog grows again.
