The A-Leagues pay dispute is heading toward a showdown

The A-Leagues and the players' union are still arguing over a fresh collective agreement after the old deal expired on 30 June. The latest proposal would raise the women's salary cap and set out a path to more full-time professionalism, but the players say the process has damaged trust and that the league moved too fast in public. That matters because the dispute affects wages, season stability and the future shape of the women's competition. The next step is for players to examine the offer and decide whether industrial action stays on the table.
