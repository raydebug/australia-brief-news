Sydney Airport runway near-miss triggers a fresh ATSB safety probe

The Australian Transport Safety Bureau is investigating a runway near-miss at Sydney Airport after two Qantas aircraft were cleared into the same area and stopped before colliding. Investigators are looking at coordination, staffing, fatigue and procedures, with no injuries or damage reported. It matters because Sydney is the country's busiest airport and one close call can expose wider pressure on the system. The next step is the investigation report and any operating changes that follow.
