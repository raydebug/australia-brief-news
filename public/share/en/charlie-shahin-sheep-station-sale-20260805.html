Charlie Shahin expands into a huge South Australian sheep aggregation

Billionaire Charlie Shahin has bought nearly 1.3 million hectares of sheep country in South Australia's northwest, including Commonwealth Hill, Mobella and Bulgunnia. The deal matters because it places one of the country's biggest pastoral aggregations under a family already known for major South Australian retail and investment interests. It also shows that large agricultural assets are still changing hands even as succession and capital pressures reshape the sector. The next question is how the new owner runs the stations and what it means for wool and sheep production in the region.
