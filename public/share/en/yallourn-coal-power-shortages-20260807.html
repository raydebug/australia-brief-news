Victoria's Yallourn closure reignites doubts about power supply

Victoria's coal exit is back under pressure after fresh warnings that the Yallourn closure could leave supply short from 2028. The debate now turns on whether enough replacement generation and storage will be ready in time. It matters because Yallourn has long been a major part of the state's baseload power. The next step is whether the closure timetable and investment pipeline can be aligned.
