A Gold Coast pub assault case has left a victim in an induced coma

Police allege a man punched Ross Burckhardt outside a Coomera pub, leaving him in an induced coma, and a magistrate has refused bail to the accused. The alleged attack happened in the car park of the Boathouse Tavern, and investigators say the suspect left in a rideshare before being arrested later. It matters because the case is another severe Queensland violence matter that has left a family waiting for answers.
