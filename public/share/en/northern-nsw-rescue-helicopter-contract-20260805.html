Northern NSW rescue helicopter contract faces fresh scrutiny

A parliamentary inquiry has put the Northern NSW rescue helicopter service under renewed pressure, with serious concerns raised about bullying, abuse and how the service is run. The committee says NSW Ambulance should not extend the contract unless the safety issues are satisfied. That matters because the service is part of the emergency response network across a large regional area, and any disruption could affect retrieval times and patient transfers. The NSW government now has three months to respond to the recommendation.
