Victoria orders poultry farms indoors as bird flu spreads

Victoria has ordered poultry producers with 50 or more birds to keep them indoors for at least 14 days as H5 bird flu detections rise. The rule covers metropolitan Melbourne, the coast and some nearby districts, and officials say the aim is to keep the virus out of commercial flocks. It matters because a spill into farms would hit egg supply, free-range labels and poultry businesses across the state. The next question is whether the precaution holds or whether more restrictions follow if wild-bird cases keep rising.
