Tasmanian worker is jailed after stealing $220,000 for online bets

A Hobart court heard a Salmon Tasmania worker stole $220,000 from his employer over three months and lost the money on online betting. The case matters because it shows how quickly gambling harm can turn into major workplace theft. He has been jailed for six months, with repayment issues still to be sorted out.
