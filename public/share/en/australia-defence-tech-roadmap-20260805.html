Australia maps out a decade of defence tech priorities

Australia has set out a 10-year defence innovation roadmap built around six priorities: long-range strike, high-energy lasers, autonomous systems, quantum technologies, artificial intelligence and undersea warfare. The point is to turn research into deployable capability faster, because recent conflicts have shown that cheap and fast-moving technology can shift the balance of power quickly. The open question is whether Defence and industry can move at that pace and deliver systems on schedule.
