Perth man charged after car-smashing spree

A 45-year-old man has been charged after an alleged 90-minute rampage through Perth's northern suburbs that damaged at least 20 parked or stationary vehicles. Police say the incident spread across several suburbs before officers brought it to an end. It matters because the damage was broad, public and risky even though no one was hurt. The next step is the court process and any explanation for what drove the spree.
