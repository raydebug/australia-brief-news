AHPRA apologises after a mass email exposed doctor details

Australia's health regulator has apologised after a bulk email exposed private details for doctors who should not have received one another's information. That matters because it goes straight to trust in the complaints system and to how regulators handle sensitive personal data. The question now is what changes AHPRA makes to prevent another disclosure and whether the incident triggers a broader privacy review.
