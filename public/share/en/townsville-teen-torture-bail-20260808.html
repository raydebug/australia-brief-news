North Queensland court hears bail case over alleged torture of a disabled teen

Police in North Queensland have put allegations about the torture of a 15-year-old intellectually disabled teenager before the Townsville courts. The case matters because it combines child protection, serious violence and any decision on electronic monitoring if bail is granted. The immediate focus is the bail hearing and the evidence supporting the conditions being sought. What happens next will determine whether the teenager is released under tight controls while the case continues.
