Replica gun scare at Sydney's Moore Park ends with two arrests

NSW Police arrested two men after a major response at Sydney's Entertainment Quarter, where officers were called over reports of a firearm. Police say the object was a replica gun, and a vest and badge were also seized. It matters because the scare shut down part of a busy precinct near the SCG and showed how quickly police will move when a weapon is reported. Inquiries are continuing and police say there is no ongoing threat.
