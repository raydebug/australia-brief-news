Katherine residents press for action after youth crime surge

Residents in Katherine are demanding stronger action after a run of youth-related thefts and dangerous joyriding. The latest scare involved youths nearly hitting a mother and her toddler, and community frustration is now spilling into calls for curfews and tougher enforcement. It matters because the town wants a response that cuts repeat offending without pushing the problem elsewhere. The next step is whether local pressure turns into changes from the NT government or police.
