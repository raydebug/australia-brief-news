Alan Jones's Sydney trial moves into detailed testimony

The Alan Jones trial in Sydney is now hearing detailed evidence from the first complainant, with the court testing allegations of indecent assault and sexual touching spanning many years. The case matters because it turns a high-profile media figure's private conduct into a long-running criminal proceeding that could reshape his public legacy. The next steps are more witness evidence, defence challenges and a judge-alone process that may run for months before any verdict or sentencing stage.
