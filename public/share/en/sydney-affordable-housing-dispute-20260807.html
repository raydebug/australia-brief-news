Sydney developer dispute tests affordable housing promises

An ABC report says Landmark Group tried to end an affordable-housing commitment at a Sydney project two years early, arguing that planning-law changes should have overridden the original deal. The state regulator had already criticised the developer’s community-housing arm over fairness and transparency with residents. The bigger issue is how much weight affordable-housing promises really carry once a project is underway. The likely next step is more regulatory and legal scrutiny, not a quick resolution.
