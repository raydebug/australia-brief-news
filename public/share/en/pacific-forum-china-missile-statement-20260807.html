Pacific forum tensions rise after China missile statement is blocked

The opening day of the Pacific Islands Forum was disrupted when Nauru and Kiribati blocked a regional statement criticising China's ballistic missile test. Australia is watching closely because the dispute cuts across security, diplomacy and influence in the Pacific. It matters as the region tries to balance unity with pressure from major powers. The next step is whether forum leaders can salvage a shared message or whether the disagreement deepens.
