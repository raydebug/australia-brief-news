Two Iranian footballers become Australian citizens after asylum route

Fatemeh Pasandideh and Atefeh Ramezanisadeh have been sworn in as Australian citizens in Brisbane after seeking asylum during the women's Asian Cup earlier this year. Both had played for Iran's women's team, stayed on humanitarian visas and later linked up with Brisbane Roar. It matters because a high-profile asylum case has now turned into permanent settlement in Queensland. The next step is whether they can keep building their lives and football careers here.
