SA Health widens Tony Modra record audit to nine staff

SA Health says a ninth staff member is now under investigation after someone inappropriately accessed Tony Modra's medical records following his truck crash and hospital treatment. The case matters because it points to how easily privacy failures can spread beyond one person and become a broader trust problem in the health system. The department says audits caught the access and disciplinary action remains possible. The next step is the investigation itself and whether SA Health tightens record controls.
