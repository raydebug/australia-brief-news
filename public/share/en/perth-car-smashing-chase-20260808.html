Perth police charge man after a car-smashing chase

Police in Perth say a 45-year-old man got out of his car, smashed several parked vehicles and then led officers on a chase through the city's north. The case matters because it mixed property damage with a public-safety response in a busy suburban area. Investigators now need to work through the alleged sequence of events and any reason behind it. The next step is the court process.
