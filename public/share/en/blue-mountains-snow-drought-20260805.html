Snow is fading from Sydney's mountain winters as warm seasons stretch on

New reporting from the Blue Mountains says NSW is in a run of warmer-than-average winters and that reliable snow cover is becoming much less common. The story matters because the mountains have long been a visible sign of southern Australia's winter climate, and a longer warm spell changes tourism, ecology and water outlooks. The next question is whether this is a short weather pattern or another sign of a deeper climate shift. For now, the pattern points to fewer cold days and less dependable snow than past generations expected.
