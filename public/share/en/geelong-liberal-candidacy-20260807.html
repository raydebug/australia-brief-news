Geelong Liberal candidacy triggers local backlash

A prominent Geelong LGBTQIA+ figure is facing criticism after being selected as a Liberal candidate for Victoria's next state election. The row is local but politically sensitive because the candidate's public profile has collided with party politics in a seat where community support matters. That matters because candidate choice can shape how quickly a campaign gains or loses trust. The next step is whether the party sticks with the nomination or tries to cool the dispute.
