Gold Coast assault victim remains critical after pub attack

A Gold Coast man remains in a critical condition after an alleged pub assault, and a magistrate has refused bail to the accused while the case proceeds. Police say the incident at the Coomera Boathouse Tavern left the victim in a coma, which has turned the matter into both a criminal case and a serious public-safety story. The scale of the injuries will shape the court process and any further charges. The immediate question is whether the accused is linked to any wider violent behaviour.
