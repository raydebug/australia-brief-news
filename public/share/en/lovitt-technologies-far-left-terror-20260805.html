Melbourne defence firebombing shifts into a terror probe

Police and ASIO are treating a July 2025 arson attack on Lovitt Technologies in Melbourne as a possible far-left terrorist act. The case matters because it is the first time Australian authorities have framed this kind of anti-weapon vandalism as terrorism, which raises the legal bar for any prosecution. Investigators say the motive appears tied to the Israel-Gaza conflict and are still trying to identify everyone involved. The next step is whether the evidence is strong enough to support terrorism charges.
