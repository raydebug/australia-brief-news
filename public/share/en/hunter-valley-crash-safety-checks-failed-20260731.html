Hunter Valley crash report finds safety checks failed

A transport report into the deadly Hunter Valley wedding bus crash says breakdowns in driver oversight, drug testing and passenger protection all played a part. The investigation says Brett Button should not have been driving that night, when the bus overturned near Greta and killed 10 people, with 25 others injured. The case matters because the report points to system failures, not just one driver's decisions, and to wider gaps in coach safety regulation. Governments and operators are now expected to work through the recommendations, but the real test is whether the new rules are enforced.
