Borrowers are waiting on the RBA's next rate call

Millions of borrowers are waiting for the Reserve Bank's Tuesday decision, with the cash rate expected to stay at 4.35 per cent but another move still on the table. Inflation, unemployment and household spending are the key signals, and recent figures show price pressure has eased only a little. That matters because even a hold keeps mortgage pressure high, while a hike would add another hit to household budgets. The next step is the board's announcement and any clue about the rest of the year.
