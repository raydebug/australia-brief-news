Ed Husic heads home from hospital after motorway crash leaves fractures

Labor MP Ed Husic has been discharged from hospital after a truck hit his car on Sydney's M7 motorway. He came away with abrasions, fractures and a concussion, and will take a short break from parliamentary duties while he recovers. It matters because a routine drive turned into a serious injury case on a major commuter route, and police are still being briefed. He is expected back in parliament once doctors say he is ready.
