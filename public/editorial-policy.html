Editorial Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

4News aims to provide concise, neutral, multi-source Australian news briefs. The site prioritises clarity, source transparency, and links to original reporting.

Source selection

4News uses public news sources relevant to Australia. Sources may include national, state, business, public, commercial, and community-focused publishers. Source inclusion does not imply endorsement.

AI summarisation

Summaries are generated by AI from available source information. The system is instructed to rewrite facts rather than copy article text, avoid unsupported claims, and preserve original links.

Multi-source grouping

Related reports may be grouped into one news item when they appear to describe the same event or issue. When sources frame a story differently, 4News may include a short source-difference note.

Corrections

If a summary appears inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading, it should be corrected or removed after review. A dedicated corrections page is available for issue reporting guidance.

Limitations

AI summaries can miss context, over-compress nuance, or reflect source errors. Readers should treat 4News as a briefing layer and consult original sources before relying on any item.
