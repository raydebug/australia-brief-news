SA Health widens its Tony Modra records probe

SA Health says its investigation into access to former footballer Tony Modra's patient records has expanded to a ninth staff member. The issue matters because it goes to the handling of private health information inside the public system and whether the original breach was broader than first thought. The department is still working through who accessed the file and why. The next step is likely to be more internal findings and possible disciplinary action.
