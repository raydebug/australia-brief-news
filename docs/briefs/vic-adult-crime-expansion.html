Victorian opposition adds more youth-crime offences to its election pitch

The Victorian opposition says it would broaden Adult Crime, Adult Time laws if it wins government, adding five more offences such as car theft and attempted carjacking. Children aged 14 and over already face adult sentencing for serious crimes under the current reforms. The issue matters because youth crime is shaping the state election debate and the opposition is betting voters want tougher penalties. The key question is whether the policy survives scrutiny from legal groups and, if elected, can be put into practice.
