Australia looks to Germany as ISIS prosecutions advance

Australia is preparing to test its own ISIS-related prosecutions, and Germany's experience is being used as a reference point. The comparison matters because war-crimes and genocide cases rely on complex evidence, long timelines and careful coordination between investigators and courts. If Australia gets the process right, it could set a durable standard for future cases. The next step is whether the hearings stay on track and produce clear legal precedents.
