Australians urged to check for $274 million in unpaid Medicare benefits

Services Australia says 974,000 people are owed $274.5 million in unpaid Medicare benefits, with the average claim about $280. The money is sitting there because bank details were not updated. That matters because many people can recover the payment quickly once their Medicare account is fixed. The exact amount will keep moving as claims are lodged and records are updated.
