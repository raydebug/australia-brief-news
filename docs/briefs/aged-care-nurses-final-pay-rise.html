Aged-care nurses receive their final scheduled pay bump

Aged-care nurses are getting the final scheduled award increase in their first full pay period from today, with enrolled nurses set to gain an extra $8,000 a year. The rise matters because the sector is still struggling to recruit and keep staff even as demand for care keeps growing. The government says the wage campaign has now lifted some nurses' pay by up to $36,000 since 2022. The next test is whether the extra money eases the shortage quickly enough to improve care.
