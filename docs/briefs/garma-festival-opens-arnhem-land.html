Garma opens with land and fuel pledge in Arnhem Land

The Garma festival has opened in north-east Arnhem Land, where the Northern Territory chief minister used her first address to promise land and fuel security after Rio Tinto's planned departure from Gove. The gathering matters because Garma often feeds into national debate on Indigenous policy, remote services and economic development. The next question is which commitments turn into practical action once the speeches end.
