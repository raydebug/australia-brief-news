Electric and hybrid cars keep gaining share in Australia

New vehicle sales data show battery electric, hybrid and plug-in hybrid models are now close to half of Australia's market. Lower running costs and a wider choice of models are helping pull buyers away from petrol-only cars, even as fuel prices stay volatile. The shift matters because it will shape fuel demand, charging demand and what carmakers prioritise here. The next test is whether prices, charging access and supply keep the trend moving.
