First Nations ties are moving into Australia's Pacific diplomacy

Australia is putting First Nations relationships closer to the centre of its Pacific diplomacy, with regional leaders backing a more culturally grounded approach. That matters because treaties, security talks and development deals rely on trust as much as money. The next question is how much of that language turns into practical policy over the coming months.
