Queensland's Victoria Park Olympic plan moves into the next phase

Queensland has unveiled the latest concept for the Brisbane 2032 precinct at Victoria Park, including a new stadium, a national aquatic centre, bridges and expanded public space. The plan matters because it sets the shape of the Games footprint and the future use of a major inner-city park. Supporters say it will leave a lasting legacy, while critics say the site is expensive, awkward to access and still short on detail about transport and crowd handling. The next step is whether the budget and early works stay on track.
