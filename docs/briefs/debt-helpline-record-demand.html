More Australians are turning to the National Debt Helpline

The National Debt Helpline says demand has hit record levels as more people seek financial counselling. That matters because it is a sign that cost-of-living pressure is reaching households that may not normally ask for help. The next question is whether the demand stays high through the coming months and whether borrowers can get relief before debts become unmanageable.
