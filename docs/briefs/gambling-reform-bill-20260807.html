Labor's gambling ad crackdown meets Senate resistance

Labor's plan to tighten gambling advertising is facing stronger resistance in the Senate, with the Coalition, Greens and crossbench senators pushing for tougher rules. The dispute centres on inducements, ad limits and whether the bill is strong enough to cut gambling harm. That matters because the outcome will decide how betting ads appear on TV, radio, sport and online. The government may need major changes before the bill can pass.
