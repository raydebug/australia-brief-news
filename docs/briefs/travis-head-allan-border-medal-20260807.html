Travis Head takes a second straight Allan Border Medal

Travis Head has won the Allan Border Medal for a second straight year after another dominant season with Australia. His three Ashes centuries and strong all-format run kept him ahead of Alex Carey and Mitchell Starc in the voting. It matters because the medal is still the clearest marker of Australia's top male cricketer across formats. The remaining question is how Cricket Australia will present the award next time after criticism of the ceremony format.
