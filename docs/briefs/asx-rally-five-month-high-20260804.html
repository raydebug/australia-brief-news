Australian shares climb to a five-month high

The Australian share market climbed sharply, pushing the ASX 200 to its highest level in five months. Banks, technology and healthcare stocks led the move, while the Australian dollar also held up well. The rally suggests investors have eased back from worries about rates and short-term growth, at least for now. The next test is whether company results and offshore markets can keep the momentum going.
