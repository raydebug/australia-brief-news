Regional Victoria dog attack sends child to hospital

A young girl has been taken to hospital after being bitten by a dog inside a Bunnings store in regional Victoria. The dog was later surrendered and euthanised, and authorities are still investigating what happened. The incident matters because it raises questions about safety in busy retail spaces and how local rules handle dangerous dogs.
