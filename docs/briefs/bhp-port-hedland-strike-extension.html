BHP faces another Port Hedland shutdown as Pilbara bargaining tightens

BHP's Port Hedland operations are heading toward another 24-hour strike, with unions also threatening a loading ban if talks fail before the deadline. The dispute matters because the terminal ships huge volumes of iron ore every week, so even a short stoppage can affect revenue, royalties and export timing. BHP says it is still working toward a fair deal, while the unions say months of slow bargaining left workers with little choice. The next step is the Fair Work Commission meeting and the August 9 deadline that could decide whether the conflict escalates again.
