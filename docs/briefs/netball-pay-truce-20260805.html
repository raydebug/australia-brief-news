Australia's Super Netball players have struck a short-term pay truce

Australia's Super Netball players have accepted a new 12-month deal that improves pay and time off after a long standoff. The agreement matters because it affects player retention, league stability and the wider push for revenue sharing in women's sport. The next negotiation will decide whether this is a temporary fix or the start of a more durable model.
