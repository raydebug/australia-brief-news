Unapproved peptides are being sold online and sent through the post

Australian health authorities are warning that unapproved peptide products are being advertised on social media and moved through ordinary parcel channels. The products are not approved for use in Australia, yet they are being marketed for weight loss, muscle building and appearance claims. That matters because the substances have not been properly assessed for safety, quality or effectiveness, and the risks can include allergic reactions, liver injury and other harm. The next step is whether regulators and platform operators can shut down the sales faster.
