NSW growers train pet dogs to hunt a more valuable truffle crop

Truffle growers in New South Wales are training family dogs to help locate black truffles as harvest demand rises. That matters because truffles are expensive to find and the dogs can make a small rural industry more productive. The open question is how much the crop will yield this season and whether more growers can turn the value of the harvest into a steadier business.
