A stolen-car attempt leaves a Melbourne man fighting for his life

A Melbourne man is in a life-threatening condition after he was hit by his own car while trying to stop it being stolen. Police say the incident happened in Heidelberg West, and the victim was taken to hospital with serious injuries. It matters because a routine car theft can become a violent attack in seconds, leaving families and police dealing with far more than a property crime. Investigators are still looking for the person who drove off.
