Australia sends fire crews and aircraft to help France fight megafires

Australia is sending a Chinook helicopter, a support aircraft and firefighters to France as major wildfires keep spreading across parts of western Europe. French and Spanish crews are still battling fast-moving blazes after a hot, dry stretch, and the Australian deployment adds aerial lift and night-fighting support. It matters because Australia is exporting hard-earned fire capability while the northern hemisphere is in another severe fire season. The timing and length of the mission will depend on how the fire fronts develop on the ground.
