Tom Dearden rejects Cowboys exit speculation

Tom Dearden has dismissed talk that he wants out of the Cowboys, saying he remains committed to North Queensland and plans to see out his contract through 2029. The story matters because the rumours have followed him through injury and family plans, turning one player decision into a wider contract story. For now, his position is unchanged: he says he is staying, and the next question is whether the noise around his future finally settles.
