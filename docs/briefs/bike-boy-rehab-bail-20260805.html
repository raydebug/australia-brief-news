Accused carjacker gets rehab bail under strict conditions

Ryan Meuleman, the man once nicknamed "bike boy" after an earlier crash involving Daniel Andrews, has been granted bail to a residential rehab facility. In court, he was accused of trying to take a car while a woman and her two young children were nearby, and he was already on bail for separate allegations. The case matters because it ties together drug treatment, repeat offending and public supervision in one ruling. He will remain under strict conditions, and the next check is whether the facility can monitor him properly.
