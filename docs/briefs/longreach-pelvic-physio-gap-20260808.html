Longreach women face longer trips after a pelvic physio leaves

Women in Longreach say losing the region's only public pelvic health physiotherapist is forcing some patients to travel hours for follow-up care. The gap matters in outback Queensland because specialist services are already thin and the trip itself can become a barrier to treatment. The health service says it is recruiting, but it cannot yet promise the same specialist skills. The next step is whether the vacancy is filled with the right expertise or a generalist substitute.
