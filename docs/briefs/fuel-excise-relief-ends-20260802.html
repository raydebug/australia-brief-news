Fuel prices are set to rise as the excise cut ends

The temporary fuel tax relief has ended, so petrol and diesel prices in Australia are expected to rise over the coming days. The change will not hit every bowser at once because retailers still have older stock to clear, but the tax burden has already returned to normal at wholesale level. It matters because it feeds straight into household commuting costs and freight bills. The next thing to watch is how quickly the higher tax is passed through and whether regulators can curb any unjustified price hikes.
