HSBC begins winding down its Australian retail banking arm

HSBC has started a phased exit from Australia's retail banking market, closing all 19 branches over the next 18 months and moving its home-loan and personal-loan portfolio into sale or transfer. The bank says its corporate, institutional and private banking services will continue. It matters because the closure removes another in-person option for customers and shows how hard it is for foreign banks to hold a retail footprint in Australia. The immediate issue is how branches, staff and home-loan customers are handled while the transition is approved and carried out.
