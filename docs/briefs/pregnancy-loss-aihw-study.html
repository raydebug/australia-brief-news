New AIHW data shows pregnancy loss is common in Australia

A new AIHW study says pregnancy loss is much more common than many people realise, with about one in three women who gave birth in 2022 having previously experienced a loss. The report is based on data from five states and territories and suggests the true scale of the issue still is not fully captured. That matters because better data and support can change how families are cared for and how stigma is reduced. The next step is whether health services use the findings to improve counselling, follow-up care and record keeping.
