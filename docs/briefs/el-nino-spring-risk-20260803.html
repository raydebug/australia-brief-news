Spring forecast points to hotter, drier conditions across Australia

Australia is heading into a hotter, drier run into spring, with El Niño adding to the risk of drought and fire weather across much of the country. The warning matters because rainfall shortfalls would hit farming, water storage and emergency planning first, especially in the south and east. Forecasts can still shift, but the signal is strong enough that authorities and households should prepare early.
