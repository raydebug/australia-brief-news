Bird flu worries rise after local transmission is confirmed

Authorities have confirmed local transmission of H5 bird flu, which raises the risk that the outbreak could spread further. The immediate work is containment, testing and protection for farms and wildlife sites. The main question now is whether this stays local or becomes a wider seasonal threat.
