Health regulator apologises after doctors were exposed in a mass email

Australia's health complaints regulator has apologised after a bulk email exposed the identities of more than 100 doctors, nurses and other practitioners invited to a webinar on addiction and alcohol care. The breach matters because the message linked names to a sensitive training topic and raised fresh questions about how health data is handled. AHPRA says the incident has been reported to the ombudsman and apologies have been sent to affected staff. The next step is to see whether the regulator changes its email safeguards and whether any wider privacy review follows.
