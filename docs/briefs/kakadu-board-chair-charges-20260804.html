Kakadu's board chair faces assault and domestic violence charges

Ryan Barrowei, who chairs Kakadu National Park's board of management, appeared in Katherine Local Court on aggravated assault and domestic violence charges. A suppression bid was refused, and the matter has been adjourned to 8 September. The case matters because Barrowei holds a senior role in a nationally significant park and the allegations now sit alongside that public responsibility. The next step is the September court mention and any further rulings on the evidence.
