Australia's childcare crackdown moves from warnings to funding threats

Education authorities are moving from warnings to funding threats after more than 60 childcare centres were issued notices and almost 600 services were shut in the latest quarter for failing standards. The federal government says stronger enforcement is needed to stop repeat breaches and protect children, while providers are being told they will be held to account more quickly. That matters because childcare safety has turned into a live test of regulation, funding and public trust. The immediate next step is whether more operators are named and whether any lose federal money.
