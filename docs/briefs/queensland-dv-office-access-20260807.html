Queensland DV survivor says ministerial access was withdrawn

A Queensland domestic violence survivor says she was told she could raise her case with a ministerial office, then later informed she could no longer call. The complaint matters because it goes beyond one person and raises questions about how government offices handle vulnerable people seeking help. It also lands in a wider debate about whether official processes are actually safe and responsive for people escaping abuse. The next step is likely more scrutiny of the decision and whether the office followed its own procedures.
