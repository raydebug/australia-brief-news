Tasmanian candidates say election signage rules put families at risk

Tasmania's election rules require signs, ads and pamphlets to show the true name and address of whoever produced them. Some candidates say that exposure can put families and staff at risk during a heated campaign. The issue matters because election transparency should identify political advertising without turning home addresses into a safety problem. The next step is whether election officials or lawmakers soften the rule before voting gets busier.
