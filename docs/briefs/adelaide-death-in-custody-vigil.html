Mourners gather in Adelaide after a 23-year-old dies in police custody

Family and friends have held a vigil for a 23-year-old Aboriginal man who died in police custody in Adelaide. The story matters because deaths in custody remain a deep source of distrust and grief for Aboriginal communities, and it quickly becomes a test of how police, coroners and governments respond. The immediate question is what investigators find about the arrest and the man’s final hours.
