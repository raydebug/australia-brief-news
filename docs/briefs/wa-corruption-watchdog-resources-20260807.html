WA watchdog wants more muscle for contractor oversight

Western Australia's corruption watchdog says it needs more staff and stronger powers to deal with government contractors and investigations. The warning lands as scrutiny of public procurement stays high. The next question is whether the state gives the commission more resources before the workload grows further.
