Sunshine shop assault leaves worker critical after teen arrests

Police say a 60-year-old retail worker is in a critical condition after a fight at a Sunshine store in Melbourne's west. Four teenagers were arrested after the incident, which puts youth crime and shopfront safety back in focus for the state. The key facts now are the worker's condition, what charges follow, and whether police treat the case as an isolated attack or part of a wider pattern.
