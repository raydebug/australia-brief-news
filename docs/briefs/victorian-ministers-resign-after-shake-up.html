Two senior Victorian ministers step down after the leadership shake-up

Victoria's cabinet is being reshuffled again after Harriet Shing and Lily D'Ambrosio said they will leave ministerial roles. Their exits come just days after Ben Carroll took over as premier, adding more instability to an already unsettled government. The main question now is how quickly Carroll can reset the front bench and whether the departures deepen the sense of change or just clear the way for it.
