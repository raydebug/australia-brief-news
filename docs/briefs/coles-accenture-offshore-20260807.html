Coles moves to shift office work offshore in an Accenture deal

Coles is moving ahead with an Accenture deal that will shift corporate work offshore, with finance, technology, human resources and marketing among the functions expected to change. Reporting puts the likely impact at hundreds of jobs and possibly more than a thousand, although store operations are not part of the plan. It matters because it shows how large Australian employers are using outsourcing and AI-led restructuring to cut costs. The next step is the final job count and how unions, staff and investors respond.

Source differences

  • AFR and news.com.au described the plan as a broader offshoring push that could affect up to about 1,000 jobs, while AAP focused on the confirmed deal and the office functions involved.
