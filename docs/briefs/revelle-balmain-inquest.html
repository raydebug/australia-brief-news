Revelle Balmain inquest sharpens focus on escort agency bosses

A New South Wales deputy state coroner has found evidence that the people running Revelle Balmain's escort agency knew more about what happened to her after she vanished in 1994. The latest findings keep the long-running case in the spotlight and send the matter back toward further police review. It matters because the disappearance has remained unresolved for decades and the coroner's conclusions could shape the next stage of the investigation.
