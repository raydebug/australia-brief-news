Melbourne arson case is being treated as far-left terrorism

Investigators say the attack on a Melbourne defence manufacturer last year is now being handled as possible far-left terrorism. The case centres on vehicles set alight, graffiti at the site and a video that appeared to claim responsibility while attacking the company's work with weapons makers. The shift matters because it brings counter-terror powers into a case that began as property damage. Police are still trying to identify the people involved and decide whether the video points to a wider extremist network.
