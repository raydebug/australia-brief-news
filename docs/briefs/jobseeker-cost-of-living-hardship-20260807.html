JobSeeker still leaves many Australians under pressure

Reporting on income support shows how a payment of about $68 a day can force people to skip meals, cut medicine and make rent harder to cover. Survey data cited in the story says people on JobSeeker are far more likely than the wider population to get by on one meal a day or less. The broader significance is that weak payment levels push stress onto food banks, health care and housing services. The policy question remains whether support rates can rise enough to stop that slide.
