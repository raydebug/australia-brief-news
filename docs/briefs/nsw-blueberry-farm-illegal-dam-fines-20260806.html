NSW blueberry farmers face heavy fines over an illegal dam

A NSW court has fined two blueberry farmers more than $130,000 over an illegal dam on their Mid North Coast property. The case shows how water and land use disputes are still landing in court, even on smaller regional farms. It also underlines the pressure on regulators to police local environmental rules consistently. The farmers will avoid demolishing the dam, but the penalty leaves a clear warning for other landholders.
