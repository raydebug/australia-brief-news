Optus faces Federal Court action over the 2025 triple-zero outage

Optus is being taken to the Federal Court over its September 2025 network failure that stopped many triple-zero calls from connecting. Regulators say the outage lasted for hours and affected customers across several states, with more than 600 calls unable to get through. The case matters because emergency access is a basic public-safety service, and the company could face large penalties if the claims are upheld. The next step is the legal process, while Optus says it is continuing to improve its systems.
