Sydney building collapse leaves two in serious condition

A wall and part of a building collapsed beside a petrol station in Ultimo, Sydney, sending debris across parked vehicles and leaving two people in serious condition. Emergency crews shut down the area, checked nearby properties and worked to make sure no one else was trapped. The incident matters because it happened in a busy inner-city block, where a failure like this can quickly threaten people, traffic and nearby services. The next steps are a structural assessment, a cause investigation and the release of any further safety advice.
