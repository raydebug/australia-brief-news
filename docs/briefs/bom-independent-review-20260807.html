BOM launches independent review after mounting criticism of its website and forecasts

The Bureau of Meteorology has ordered an independent review after a rough stretch that included criticism of its website, forecasting tools and communication with farmers. The review will look at whether the agency is giving Australians weather information in a form they can trust and use, especially for expensive decisions in agriculture. That matters because weather data is core infrastructure for farming, emergency planning and daily life. The next step is the review panel's report and whether it leads to website or service changes.
