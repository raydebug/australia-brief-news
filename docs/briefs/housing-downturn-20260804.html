Australia's housing downturn deepens as price falls spread to more cities

Australian house prices fell again in July, and the drop is no longer limited to Sydney and Melbourne. Cotality's numbers show a national fall of 0.7 per cent, with Brisbane and Adelaide also moving lower, while banks are recalibrating forecasts for the rest of the year. That matters for borrowing power, investor sentiment and first-home buyers waiting for cheaper entry points. The next watchpoint is whether lower rates or softer credit conditions slow the slide.
