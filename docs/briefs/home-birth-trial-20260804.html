Triple-zero evidence is aired in a Queensland home-birth manslaughter trial

A Queensland manslaughter trial has heard a triple zero call that sits at the centre of a fatal home-birth case. The evidence matters because jurors are being asked to assess what happened in the critical minutes before emergency help arrived, and whether the response met the standard required. It also raises the wider question of how home-birth emergencies are handled when complications escalate quickly. The next step is the rest of the trial and the jury's view of the timeline.
