Darwin gas plants face tougher pollution rules after emissions review

The Northern Territory EPA has recommended tighter licence conditions for Santos and Inpex after a review found major under-reporting of toxic emissions. The move matters because the Darwin gas plants sit near a major industrial port and have long been central to NT jobs, royalties and environmental concerns. The immediate question is how much the companies can change the regulator's recommendations before the licences are finalised.
