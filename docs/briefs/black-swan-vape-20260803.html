WA teens face court over black swan vape video

Two teenage boys in Western Australia have appeared in court after a video allegedly showed them making a black swan inhale vape fumes. The case matters because it mixes wildlife harm with social-media recording, and it could sharpen scrutiny of how such clips are shared. The next step is the court process and any animal welfare response.
