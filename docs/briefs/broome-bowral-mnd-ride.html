Broome-to-Bowral riders close in on the finish after a 5,300 km charity push

A grandfather and his teenage grandson are in the final stretch of a ride from Broome to Bowral to raise money and attention for motor neurone disease. The trip covers about 5,300 kilometres and has meant long early starts, heavy heat and persistent headwinds across remote parts of northern and eastern Australia. The effort matters because it is tied to research and support for people living with MND, a disease with no cure. The focus now is whether they can roll into Bowral on August 2 after pushing their fundraising total a little higher.
