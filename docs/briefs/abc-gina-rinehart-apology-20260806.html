ABC apology to Gina Rinehart deepens the broadcaster's satire row

The ABC has apologised to Gina Rinehart after a satirical Race Around the World segment triggered complaints and was removed from its platforms. The broadcaster's ombudsman said the episode did not breach editorial standards, but management said it still failed to meet community expectations and promised policy changes. The row matters because it puts satire, editorial judgment and broadcaster standards under the spotlight at the same time. The next step is the internal review, which will decide whether the ABC tightens approval rules for controversial material.
