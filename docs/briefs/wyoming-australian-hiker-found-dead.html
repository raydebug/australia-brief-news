Australian hiker found dead after a search in Wyoming

A 28-year-old Australian hiker has been found dead in Wyoming after going missing on the Continental Divide Trail. The case matters because it ends a long search and highlights how quickly mountain conditions can turn deadly even for experienced trekkers. Authorities think he likely fell on a steep ridgeline, but the exact sequence of events still rests on the investigation.
