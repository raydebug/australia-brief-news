Alex Ryvchin portrait wins the Archibald People's Choice award

Michael Zavros has won the Archibald People's Choice award for his portrait of Jewish community leader Alex Ryvchin. The painting was made after the Bondi terror attack and drew a record number of public votes, giving the prize its strongest audience response yet. The result matters because it ties a major arts award to wider questions of grief, identity and belonging. The exhibition remains open through mid-August.
