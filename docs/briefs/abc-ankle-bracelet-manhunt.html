Police hunt eight alleged offenders after ankle bracelets are cut off

Police say eight alleged offenders are on the run after their electronic monitoring devices were cut off, forcing a big manhunt to pull officers away from other duties. The case matters because monitoring only works if the devices stay on and alert police when they do not. The next step is finding them and figuring out how the devices were defeated.
