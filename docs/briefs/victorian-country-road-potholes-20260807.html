Victorian residents are helping mark dangerous country-road potholes

A man in regional Victoria has been getting donations of money and paint from locals who want to help make potholes more visible on crumbling country roads. The grassroots effort has filled a gap while drivers wait for repairs that are still behind the damage. It matters because rough roads can damage vehicles and create real safety risks for rural communities. The question now is whether the pressure speeds up maintenance or just adds temporary fixes.
