John Gibbs steps back after cancer diagnosis

Former Manly-Warringah Sea Eagles halfback and ABC Sport commentator John Gibbs says he has been diagnosed with Hodgkin's lymphoma. He is stepping away from broadcasting while he starts treatment, with support already building from colleagues and fans. It matters because Gibbs has been a familiar voice in rugby league for years, so his diagnosis has landed widely across the sport. His next step is treatment, and no public prognosis has been given.
