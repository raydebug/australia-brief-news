Cairns stolen-car crash brings murder allegations

Police in Cairns say a stolen-car crash killed 25-year-old Savannah Spence and are now seeking murder allegations against the accused driver. The case also involves claims that he fled the scene and later carjacked another driver, which has sharpened the bail debate in Queensland. It matters because one fatal crash has widened into a broader public-safety case. The next step is the court process and any further police allegations.
