High Court gives native title holders a win in the NT water fight

Native title holders in Central Australia have won a High Court outcome over a huge groundwater licence for a horticulture project near Tennant Creek. The licence would allow Fortune Agribusiness to pump 40 gigalitres a year from Singleton Station for 30 years, a scale local owners say threatens sacred sites and desert water systems. The decision matters because it is another test of how Australia balances large-scale agricultural development with native title rights and cultural heritage. The next step is the full legal and administrative fallout for the licence and the project.
