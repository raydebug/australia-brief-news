Australia's cheap food supply still rests on vulnerable labour

Australia's low-cost food supply is still being supported by workers who carry the least protection, the ABC says. The reporting points to insecure hours, weak pay and labour conditions that help keep fruit and vegetables cheap for shoppers while leaving growers and packhouses with little room to lift standards. That matters because supply chains can hide exploitation behind a low supermarket price. The next question is whether enforcement and buyer pressure can improve pay and conditions without pushing food costs sharply higher.
