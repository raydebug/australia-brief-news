A telehealth data breach warns more than a million Australians

Patients of a telehealth provider were warned after a cyberattack exposed personal information. The scale matters because health-related data can be used for scams, identity theft and privacy abuse long after the breach itself. The investigation now has to establish what was taken and how widely it spread.
