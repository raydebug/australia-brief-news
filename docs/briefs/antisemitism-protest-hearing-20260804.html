Antisemitism hearing shifts to protests, police handling and online radicalisation

The royal commission's latest evidence block has put Sydney protests, police handling and online radicalisation back under the microscope. Witnesses are being asked about the 2023 Opera House rally, the 2025 neo-Nazi protest outside NSW parliament and the way hate can move from digital spaces into public confrontation. The issue matters because the inquiry is now looking beyond single incidents to the systems around policing, platforms and youth radicalisation. The next step is more testimony and possible recommendations on how Australia should prevent the same pattern from repeating.
