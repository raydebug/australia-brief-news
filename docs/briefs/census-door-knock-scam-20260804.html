ABS warns Australians to watch for census impostors

The ABS is warning people to be careful about fake census workers who may try to knock on doors before the August 11 count. Real staff should never ask for bank details, passwords or other sensitive data. That matters because the census feeds school, health and infrastructure planning, so scams can damage trust before the survey even starts. The next step is more public reminders and prompt reporting of suspicious visits.
