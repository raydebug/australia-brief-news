South-east Queensland is due a brief sunny break before a windy Ekka week

South-east Queensland is getting a short spell of better weather before a windier and less settled pattern arrives for Ekka week. That matters for travel, outdoor events and the crowds heading to Brisbane's showground. Forecasters will now watch how strong the change becomes and whether it disrupts plans.
