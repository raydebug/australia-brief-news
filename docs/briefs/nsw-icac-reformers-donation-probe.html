NSW ICAC keeps pressure on the Liberals' Reformers faction over alleged donations and invoices

The NSW corruption watchdog has kept pressure on the Liberal Party's Reformers faction by airing claims about hidden donations, fake invoices and factional deals. The hearing has repeatedly drawn Angus Taylor's name into the discussion, although he denies making any payment. The case matters because it goes to party funding rules, internal power plays and public trust in the political system. The inquiry is still running, so more evidence and denials are likely before it finishes.
