Queensland writes off almost $140m in unpaid fines

Queensland wrote off almost $140 million in unpaid fines last financial year, covering more than 460,000 debts. That matters because fines become a policy problem when the state collects less and the amounts keep stacking up. The next step is whether officials tighten recovery or rethink penalties that are proving hard to collect.
