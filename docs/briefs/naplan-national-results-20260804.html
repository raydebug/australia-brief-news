NAPLAN results point to a national literacy and numeracy slide

Australia's latest NAPLAN release points to a broader slide in reading, writing and maths, with a large share of students still missing benchmark levels. One report highlights one-third of students falling short in literacy and numeracy, while another focuses on a softer reading and writing picture. The issue matters because these results feed into school support, policy debates and the resources children need before secondary school. The next question is whether states can turn the data into targeted help before the gap widens further.
