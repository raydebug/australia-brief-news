Airtrain heads to court over Queensland rail pricing

Airtrain says Queensland government pricing changes have cut about A$6 million from its revenue, and it is taking the dispute to court. The case matters because airport rail is caught between public transport policy and the finances of a private operator. What happens next will depend on the court timeline and whether either side changes its position before a ruling.
