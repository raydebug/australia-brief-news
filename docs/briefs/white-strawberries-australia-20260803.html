Australian growers trial white and blush strawberries

Australian horticulture is now growing white and blush strawberry varieties commercially, not just the familiar red fruit. The new berries matter because growers are looking for products that stand out and can hold value in the market. The next test is whether shoppers accept the colour change and whether the varieties can scale beyond a niche.
