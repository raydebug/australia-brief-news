Families return to the St Basil's inquest as memories and records are tested

A coronial inquest into the St Basil's aged care tragedy has resumed on the anniversary of the outbreak that left 50 residents dead from Covid. Family members say the loss is still raw, while some managers have struggled to recall what happened. The case matters because it examines how an aged care home failed during one of the worst moments of the pandemic. The next stage is more evidence about the decisions, records and responsibilities behind the deaths.
