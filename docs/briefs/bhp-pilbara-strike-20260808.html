BHP strike threatens Pilbara iron ore shipments

Workers at BHP's Port Hedland export terminal have walked off the job again in a two-day dispute over pay and conditions. The stoppage covers ship loading and other work at Australia's biggest bulk iron ore port, where around 150 workers are involved. The mine exported hundreds of millions of tonnes last financial year, so even a short disruption can ripple through BHP's earnings, state royalties and overseas customers. Talks are due to resume on August 18, and both sides are still far apart on the size and timing of a pay deal.
