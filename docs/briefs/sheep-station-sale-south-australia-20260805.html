Vast SA sheep station changes hands in major sale

One of Australia's biggest contiguous sheep stations has changed hands after Jumbuck Pastoral sold Commonwealth Hill to Adelaide businessman Charlie Shahin. The sale matters because it concentrates a huge stretch of South Australian outback into private ownership and underlines how valuable large-scale pastoral land remains. The property covers nearly 1.3 million hectares and sits in a region where scale, water and logistics all drive value. The next question is how the new owner uses the land and whether the deal shifts local employment or production.
