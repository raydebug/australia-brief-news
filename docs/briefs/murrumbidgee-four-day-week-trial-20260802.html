A NSW council is testing a four-day week

Murrumbidgee Council plans to trial a four-day working week from next year, turning the change into 52 long weekends for staff if it goes ahead as planned. It is an Australian first and will be watched closely for signs that shorter hours can improve recruitment, retention and morale without hurting services. The key test will be whether the trial works in practice, not just on paper.
