ACFS Port Logistics is pushed into administration

ACFS Port Logistics has entered administration, putting a major container logistics operator under insolvency control. The company handles freight through ports across the country and serves large retail and industrial customers. It matters because any disruption can flow into supply chains, worker jobs and creditor recoveries. The administrator will now assess assets, debts and options for a sale or restructuring.
