Rhiannan Iffland seals a 10th straight cliff diving title

Australian diver Rhiannan Iffland has won the penultimate event of the World Series in Bosnia and locked up her 10th straight title. The result takes her to 48 career wins and puts her at the top of the sport's all-time list. That matters because it marks a decade of near-total dominance in one of diving's most dangerous disciplines, with one event still left this season. The final stop in Italy is now about whether she can stretch the record even further.
