Meta is paying some Australian creators tied to rage-bait content

An ABC investigation says Meta is directly paying several controversial Australian content creators, including people linked to white nationalist and anti-vaccine material. The issue matters because it shows a major platform may be monetising content that sits close to its own rules on harmful engagement. The central question is whether these payments are isolated or part of a broader problem in how social media rewards outrage. More scrutiny is likely as the company faces pressure over moderation and monetisation standards.
