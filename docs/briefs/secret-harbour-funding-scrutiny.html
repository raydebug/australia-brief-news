Fresh campaign cash in Secret Harbour triggers pork-barrelling concerns

New funding promises in Western Australia’s Secret Harbour race have prompted questions about whether campaign spending is crossing into pork-barrelling. The timing has put attention on how public money is being announced before voters head to the polls. That matters because election-period promises can blur the line between policy and politics, especially in tight contests. The unresolved point is whether the spending details will stand up once they are examined.
