Victoria widens bird flu checks after more suspected H5N1 cases

Victoria's bird flu response is widening after more suspected H5N1 cases turned up in wildlife. Authorities are expanding testing and biosecurity checks around affected sites to see how far the virus has spread. The risk to people remains low, but the issue matters for wildlife health, poultry planning and any further restrictions if the outbreak grows. The next step is confirmation testing and surveillance for new cases.
