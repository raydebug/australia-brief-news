Queensland's major-project bill has reignited a fight over farmland and planning powers

A Queensland bill meant to speed up major projects has sparked concern among farming families who fear productive land could be pushed aside too easily. The debate matters because it sits between housing, infrastructure and food production, and it will decide how much weight local objections carry. The key question now is what changes are made before the bill moves forward.
