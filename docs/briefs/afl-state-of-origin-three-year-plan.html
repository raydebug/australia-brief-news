AFL locks in a three-year State of Origin return with matches in WA and South Australia

The AFL has confirmed that State of Origin football will continue for another three years. Western Australia will host South Australia in 2027, South Australia will host Victoria in 2028, and WA will meet Victoria again in 2029. The plan matters because it gives the revived concept a clear runway after a strong return and keeps state rivalries on the calendar. The next test is whether crowds and clubs stay committed enough for the series to last beyond that window.
