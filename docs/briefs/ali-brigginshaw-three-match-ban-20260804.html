Ali Brigginshaw's tackle case ends in a three-game ban

Ali Brigginshaw has been suspended for three games after the NRLW judiciary found her tackle on Canterbury's Tayla Preston was dangerously executed. The ruling matters because it removes one of Brisbane's key leaders during the run home and shows the league is treating head-first landings more harshly. The next question is how the Broncos reshuffle their side and whether the decision becomes a tougher benchmark for similar tackles.
