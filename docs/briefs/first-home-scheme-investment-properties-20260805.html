Some first-home scheme purchases have become investment properties

New data shows almost 1,500 homes bought through the federal 5% deposit scheme were later converted into investment properties. The scheme was designed to help first-time buyers get a foothold with less upfront cash, but the figures show some participants have moved on from owner-occupation. That matters because it feeds the wider argument over housing affordability and whether the policy is helping the right households. The unresolved question is whether the government tightens eligibility, monitoring or disclosure rules.
