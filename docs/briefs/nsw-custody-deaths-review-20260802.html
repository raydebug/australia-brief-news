New custody death figures are deepening pressure on NSW prison reform

Fresh figures showing a record number of First Nations deaths in NSW prison custody have sharpened pressure on the state to act. The numbers matter because they point to a systemic justice problem rather than an isolated tragedy, and advocates say the trend needs urgent oversight. The next step is whether NSW responds with stronger monitoring and reform.
