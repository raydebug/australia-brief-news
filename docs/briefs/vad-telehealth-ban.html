Australia's assisted-dying telehealth ban is back under scrutiny

The debate over telehealth for voluntary assisted dying has returned after renewed calls to let patients consult remotely. Supporters say the current ban can make access harder for people in regional areas, while opponents warn that any change must preserve strict safeguards. That matters because the rule affects both access to care and how end-of-life law is enforced across states. The next step is whether governments are willing to change the rules or keep the limits in place.
