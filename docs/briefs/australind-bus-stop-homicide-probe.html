WA police investigate a fatal bus-stop attack in Australind

Police in Western Australia are investigating the death of a man in his 20s after he was found with critical injuries near a bus stop in Australind, south of Perth. He was taken to Bunbury Hospital but could not be saved, and detectives are now treating the case as a homicide. The location matters because the incident has put attention on a busy regional corridor and the safety of public spaces after dark. The next steps are forensic checks, witness interviews and confirming exactly what happened before dawn on Sunday.
