Reporting season opens with profit rebound hopes

Australia's biggest listed companies are about to start reporting full-year results after three straight years of weaker profits. Early names include AMP, Beach Energy and ResMed, with CBA and Telstra due next week, so investors are watching whether higher fuel costs, wages and tighter margins are easing. It matters because the results will influence the ASX, super funds and the tone of the economy through August. The next few weeks will show whether the profit cycle is finally turning.
