Broome camel tour leaves two people injured

A camel ride on Cable Beach in Broome ended badly after the animal lurched and threw two riders. ABC says a woman in her 60s was taken to hospital with injuries, and video from the scene showed the camel bucking during the sunset tour. The story matters because camel rides are a visible part of Broome's tourism economy, and visitors will be watching whether operators change safety checks or ride handling after this incident. For now, officials have not said there was a wider fault with the tour, so the main follow-up is the condition of the injured riders and any review that follows.
