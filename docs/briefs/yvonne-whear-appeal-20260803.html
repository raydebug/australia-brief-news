Murwillumbah family renews the search for Yvonne Whear

The family of Yvonne Whear has renewed its appeal for information after the 73-year-old disappeared near Murwillumbah months ago. Police say her last known movements were captured in February, and investigators still need leads on what happened after that. The case matters because the search has now stretched over half a year without answers for the family or police. The next step is public help, including any sighting of her car or movements around the Northern Rivers.
