Lidia Thorpe backs a national racism inquiry

Lidia Thorpe has backed calls for a standalone royal commission into racism against Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people. The push has grown alongside a parliamentary inquiry hearing evidence about racism in politics, media and public life. This matters because it could move the debate from testimony alone to a formal inquiry with stronger powers and broader terms. The next step is whether Canberra widens the scope or keeps the current review process.
