Dry July leaves Great Southern farmers waiting on rain

Farmers in Western Australia's Great Southern are heading into August after a very dry July, with rainfall well below normal across the region. Some growers say soil moisture is dropping fast and water stores are under pressure, especially inland where earlier falls never really arrived. The next useful rain will matter for crops, livestock and dams, but forecasters are still describing the relief as patchy.
