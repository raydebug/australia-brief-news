CFMEU inquiry keeps pressure on Mick Gatto's labour-hire links

A Queensland inquiry into the CFMEU has kept focus on claims that labour-hire companies tied to Mick Gatto were used to win access to Victorian Big Build projects. Geoffrey Watson has stood by his report and says the evidence still points to entrenched corruption and opaque company control, while the firms deny the allegations. It matters because the dispute goes to how taxpayer-funded construction work can be distorted by fixers and hidden ownership. The next step is more inquiry evidence and the Victorian royal commission now promised by Ben Carroll.
