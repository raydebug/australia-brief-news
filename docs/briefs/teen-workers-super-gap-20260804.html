Teen workers are missing out on super under an outdated rule

AAP says a long-standing rule is leaving many teenage workers without superannuation contributions, and the missed payments can compound over time. The estimate in the story is that the gap can cost more than $10,000 across a career. It matters because the shortfall starts early, hits low-paid workers hardest and can become a much bigger retirement gap later on. The next question is whether lawmakers move quickly enough to close the loophole.
