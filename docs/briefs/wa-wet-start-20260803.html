South West WA set for a wetter start after a record-dry July

The South West is set for a wetter start to August after one of the driest Julys on record. Rain matters here because it can ease short-term soil stress and help local water supplies, but it can also bring slippery roads and slow farm work. Forecasters will now watch how much of the system holds together and whether the rain reaches inland districts.
