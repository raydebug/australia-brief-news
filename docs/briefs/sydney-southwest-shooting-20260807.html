Police link a Greenacre shooting to a burning ute

Police say an 18-year-old man was shot in Greenacre and later found a burnt ute in Granville that they believe is linked. The case matters because it points to a violent incident in Sydney's south-west with a vehicle fire used to cover tracks. The next step is for detectives to piece together who was involved and why the shooting happened. For now, the injured man is in hospital and the investigation is still open.
