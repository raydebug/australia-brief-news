Luke Bateman's gambling losses put fresh focus on betting harm

Court reporting has added more detail to Luke Bateman's gambling trouble, saying he took money from his partner while she slept to keep feeding the habit. The story links the alleged theft to his role as a witness in the Senate's gambling inquiry. It matters because the case shows how addiction can spread from a private problem into family finances and public policy. The wider question is whether Australia's gambling controls can reduce that harm fast enough.
