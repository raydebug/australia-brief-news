Townsville assault case grows to 11 charges

Police in Townsville say a 17-year-old boy was allegedly assaulted, bound and left in bushland near Cape Pallarenda, and the case has now grown to 11 people charged. The case matters because investigators say the attack was not random and the violence was serious enough to trigger major child-protection concern in far north Queensland. Court proceedings are continuing, and detectives may still adjust the charge list as they test the evidence.
