Australia Post wins approval for a higher standard letter price

Australia Post has been cleared to raise the basic price of a standard letter after an ACCC review. The move matters because letter volumes keep falling, so the service is trying to cover rising losses while defending a shrinking part of its business. Customers will feel it first, while the longer-term issue is how much the postal network can be reshaped without cutting service too far.
