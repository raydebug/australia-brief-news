Older Australians brace for a bigger private health bill

The federal government is holding to its plan to remove the extra private health insurance rebate for Australians over 65, a change that would lift premiums for millions from April 2027. The policy is being sold as a move to put rebates on an income-only footing and to redirect savings toward aged care. The debate matters because opponents say some seniors will drop cover and add pressure to public hospitals, while the government says the budget trade-off is unavoidable.
