Luke Gosling case is sent to federal prosecutors

Federal Labor MP Luke Gosling is set to be handled by the Commonwealth Director of Public Prosecutions after assault charges were referred away from the NT system to avoid a conflict. The case matters because it puts a sitting MP under renewed pressure while the court process continues, with the allegations already drawing calls for him to stand aside from public duties. The legal question now is straightforward: how the prosecution proceeds, and whether the political fallout grows before the next court date.
