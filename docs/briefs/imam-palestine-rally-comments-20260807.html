Australian imam criticises rally remarks about October 7

A prominent Australian imam has criticised comments made at a pro-Palestinian rally after another speaker described October 7 as a day of pride and victory. The backlash matters because it adds to the national debate over protest language, antisemitism and public accountability. The next step is how the royal commission and political leaders respond to the criticism.
