Multicultural communities are watching the census for service funding

For many multicultural communities, the census is not just a headcount. The data helps shape funding for interpreting services, schools, public transport and local libraries, so missing answers can affect how support is distributed. That is why participation matters well beyond the form itself. The next question is whether communities trust the process enough to return accurate information and whether government agencies use the results fairly.
