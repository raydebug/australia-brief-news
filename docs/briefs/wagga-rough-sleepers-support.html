Wagga locals are propping up rough sleepers as housing pressure drags on

In Wagga Wagga, locals are stepping in to help rough sleepers while official responses lag behind the housing crisis. The Guardian says residents are sharing food, transport and health support as people at Wilks Park face pressure to move on without anywhere stable to go. It matters because the camp shows how homelessness becomes a community emergency when there are too few rentals and too little crisis accommodation. The next question is whether the council and state government can move people into safe housing before enforcement starts.
