Montag leads Australia's Commonwealth Games race-walk sweep

Jemima Montag won the women's 10,000m race walk in Glasgow after sitting behind teammate Elizabeth McMillen for most of the race and then pulling clear late to finish on top of an Australian podium sweep with Rebecca Henderson. The result matters because it adds another gold to Australia's Commonwealth Games athletics tally and shows the country's race-walking program still has real depth. The next question is how much more Australia can add as the athletics schedule moves into its closing sessions.
