Census scam warnings spread as census night nears

With the 2026 census approaching, officials and broadcasters are warning Australians about fake letters, calls and texts that claim to be from the census. The advice is simple: do not hand over personal details to unsolicited callers, and use the official ABS channels instead of clicking random links. It matters because census responses feed funding, planning and public services across the country. The next step is more public reminders before census night on August 11.
