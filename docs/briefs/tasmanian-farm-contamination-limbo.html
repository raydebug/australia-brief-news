Contamination leaves a fifth-generation Tasmanian farm in limbo

A fifth-generation farm near Launceston is stuck waiting for answers after contamination issues were found in its waterways. The ABC says poor environmental regulation has left the family with few practical options while they try to keep the farm running and protect the land. It matters because legacy pollution can freeze normal farming work for years and shift the cost onto one rural family. The next step is whether regulators or the polluter move quickly enough to give the farm a clear path forward.
