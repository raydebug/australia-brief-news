Free-range egg labels stay in place as hens move indoors

Australia's egg producers are moving hens indoors to limit bird flu risk, and the regulator has relaxed free-range labelling rules to match the change. That matters because it affects consumer expectations, food prices and biosecurity at the same time. The key question now is whether the disease pressure eases or more restrictions follow.
