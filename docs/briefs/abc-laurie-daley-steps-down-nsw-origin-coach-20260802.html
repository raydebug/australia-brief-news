Laurie Daley steps away from the NSW Origin job

Laurie Daley has stepped down as New South Wales State of Origin coach, ending a short but high-pressure stint. The move matters because Origin coaching shapes selection, preparation and the next round of interstate rivalry. The immediate question is who takes over and how quickly the Blues can settle before the next series.
