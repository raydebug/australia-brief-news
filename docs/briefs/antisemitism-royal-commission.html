Royal commission hears antisemitism concerns from arts and education sectors

A royal commission is hearing evidence about antisemitism across Australia's arts, health and education sectors. The issue matters because it links workplace safety, community trust and the broader debate about social cohesion. What happens next will depend on what the commission recommends and whether governments turn those findings into policy.
