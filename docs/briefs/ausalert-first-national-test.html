National AusAlert test shows Australia's emergency warning system can reach almost every phone

Australia ran the first nationwide test of the new AusAlert system on 27 July. The alert sent a siren and message to compatible phones, tablets and some watches, and authorities said the signal reached most targeted towers. The system matters because it is designed to warn people quickly during bushfires, floods and other fast-moving emergencies. The main question now is how well it works for older devices, weak coverage and people who need hidden phones for safety.
