Australia's fuel relief ends and pump prices start climbing

The temporary fuel excise cut ended at midnight on August 3, lifting wholesale fuel costs by 16 cents a litre and setting up higher prices at the bowser over the following days. The change matters because transport costs flow straight into household budgets, freight bills and inflation. Retailers are expected to work through cheaper stock first, so the jump should arrive gradually rather than all at once, but regulators are warning against opportunistic price rises. The next question is how quickly the increase spreads through the cities and whether the ACCC sees any sign of gouging.

Source differences

  • AAP focuses on the wholesale cost change and the immediate pressure on motorists, while Guardian and news.com.au frame it as a wider cost-of-living and inflation story. ABC explains that the retail rise should filter through over roughly 10 days.
