Perth business says Metronet works have cost it $250,000

A Perth business owner says long-running rail construction linked to Metronet has wiped about $250,000 from the bottom line. The complaint focuses on the disruption created by the works and on whether nearby traders have been fairly compensated. It matters because major transport projects can drag on local businesses long after the first diggers arrive. The next question is whether the WA government or project managers respond with any new support.
