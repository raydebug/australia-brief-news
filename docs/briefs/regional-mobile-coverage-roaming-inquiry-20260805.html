Regional mobile coverage review puts domestic roaming on the table

The ACCC's mobile coverage inquiry is now focusing on whether people in regional black spots should be able to roam on another network. That matters for emergency calls, farm businesses and long-distance travel, where a dead patch can turn into a safety problem fast. Regulators still have to decide how broad the rule should be, who would pay, and whether roaming would slow down new tower investment.
