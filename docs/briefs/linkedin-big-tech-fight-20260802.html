LinkedIn is being pulled into Australia's news bargaining fight

The federal government's media bargaining incentive is widening, with LinkedIn now expected to be caught in the rules around platforms that may pay for Australian journalism. That matters because the policy is meant to push digital giants into deals with local publishers, not just search and social networks. The next step is the final shape of the scheme and whether smaller outlets gain more support.
