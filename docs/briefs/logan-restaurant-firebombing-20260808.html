Logan restaurant firebombing raises fresh fears about arson

Police are investigating an arson attack on a Logan restaurant that was still being renovated only weeks before its planned opening. It matters because the fire hit a small business before it had even traded and adds to concern about deliberate attacks on commercial premises. The key questions now are who set it alight, why the venue was targeted and whether it is linked to any wider pattern.
