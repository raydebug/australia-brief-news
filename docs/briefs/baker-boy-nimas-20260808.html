Baker Boy collects three major NIMAs in Darwin

At Darwin's National Indigenous Music Awards, Baker Boy picked up album, song and film clip honours, while Birdz and Fred Leone were named artist of the year. The awards matter because they still track which First Nations artists are cutting through nationally, not just in their home communities. This result strengthens Baker Boy's profile after another big year of releases and live shows. The next step is the post-awards momentum around touring, streaming and festival bookings.
