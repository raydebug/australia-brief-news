Victoria apologises to public-school abuse survivors

Victoria's premier has formally apologised in parliament to people abused in state government schools, saying the state failed to protect them and failed to act on warnings. The apology follows an inquiry that examined abuse across dozens of schools and backed a wider truth-telling process and memorial. It matters because it turns a long-running scandal into an official state acknowledgement, but survivors are still waiting to see how quickly the promised reforms and support will follow.
