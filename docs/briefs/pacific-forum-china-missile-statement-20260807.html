Pacific ministers still cannot agree on a response to China's missile test

Pacific foreign ministers are still split over a statement condemning China's recent missile test, with Nauru and Kiribati blocking consensus. Australia and New Zealand want the forum to speak clearly because the issue affects regional security and how much influence Beijing has in the Pacific. It matters because the Pacific Islands Forum can only act by agreement, and that leaves little room for compromise. The next step is whether ministers can narrow the gap before leaders meet next month.
