Replica gun scare at Sydney Moore Park ends with two arrests

Two men have been arrested at Sydney's Moore Park after police responded to an incident involving a replica firearm. Officers say they seized a replica gun, a vest and a badge, and there is no ongoing threat to the public. It matters because the response unfolded in a busy public area and quickly raised safety concerns before police confirmed the object was fake. The next step is the criminal case and any explanation of why the men were there.
