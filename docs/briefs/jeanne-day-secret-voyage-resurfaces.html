Jeanne Day's secret voyage from Port Lincoln comes back into view

In 1928, South Australian teacher Jeanne Day cut her hair, dressed as a boy and slipped onto a Finnish tall ship in Port Lincoln. She wanted the passage to England and ended up leaving behind a rare local story about determination, gender rules and life at sea. The episode matters because it ties an ordinary schoolteacher to a remarkable piece of maritime history in South Australia. The remaining question is how much of Day's own account survives and how historians place the voyage in the record.
