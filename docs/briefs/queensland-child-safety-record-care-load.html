Queensland child-safety reform starts from a record care load

Queensland has released its response to the child-safety inquiry while new data shows almost 2,900 children in residential care, up sharply from last year. The government says it has accepted most of the recommendations and will phase the changes in over the next 30 months. The issue matters because the figures show how much pressure remains on the care system, and the real test will be whether reform reduces the need for residential placements.
