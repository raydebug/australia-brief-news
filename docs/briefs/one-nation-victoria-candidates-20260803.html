One Nation's Victorian launch draws scrutiny over media bans

One Nation has unveiled its first Victorian election candidates while the row over Pauline Hanson's decision to exclude ABC, The Guardian and The Age keeps widening. Warren Pickering is leading the party's push in Victoria, and the slate mixes former Liberal figures with local candidates as the party tries to build a foothold before the state election. It matters because the launch shows both the party's electoral ambitions and its effort to control the spotlight. The next step is whether it can turn the attention into a clearer policy pitch.
