Lockyer Valley quarry plans are withdrawn after farmer pressure

A sandstone quarry proposal in Queensland's Lockyer Valley has been withdrawn after pressure from local farmers. The retreat ends a land-use fight in a major agricultural district, where residents argued the project threatened farming country and the wider landscape. It matters because the case sits at the intersection of resource projects, food production and local consent. The next step is whether the company returns with a revised plan or walks away entirely.
