A Tasmanian racism inquiry hears how one photo sparked online abuse

A federal inquiry into racism, hate and violence toward First Nations people heard in Hobart that a social media photo quickly drew racist abuse. Witnesses described comments about skin colour and Aboriginal identity, showing how fast online pile-ons can escalate around public posts. It matters because the hearing is building a record of how racism spreads and how much harm social media abuse can cause. The next step is more evidence as the inquiry continues through Hobart and other cities.
