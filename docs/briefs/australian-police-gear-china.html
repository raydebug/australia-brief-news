Australian police gear sold online in China prompts scam warnings

Australian police uniforms, caps, badges and border-force gear have been found on Chinese online shopping sites. The risk is not just embarrassment: officials warn that the items could help impersonation scams or make fake police contacts look convincing. The key issue is how much protection public agencies can get once official symbols are copied and sold offshore. Police say they will keep watching the listings and warning the public.
