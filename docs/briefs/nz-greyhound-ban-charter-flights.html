Greyhounds begin arriving in Australia as New Zealand's racing ban starts

New Zealand has started winding down greyhound racing, and dogs from the industry are now being moved into Australia for rehoming and care. That matters because rescue groups here will have to absorb more animals while the sport shrinks across the Tasman. The immediate test is how many dogs need homes and whether welfare groups have enough capacity before the ban fully takes hold.
