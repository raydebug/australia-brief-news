Labor's gambling ad crackdown faces a Senate fight

Labor's gambling ad bill is running into resistance from the Coalition, the Greens and crossbench senators. The proposal would cut back on advertising in some broadcast windows and tighten rules around promotions and inducements, but critics want stronger action. This matters because gambling harm, media revenue and Senate numbers are all colliding at once. The open question is whether ministers compromise, shelve the bill or push ahead with a weaker version.
