Rinehart pushes back over the ABC ruling on her complaint

Gina Rinehart has criticised the ABC ombudsman finding after a Race Around the World segment aired a fictional attack scenario involving her. The dispute turns on whether the broadcaster handled a sensitive subject with enough care and whether its editorial standards need tightening. It matters because it tests how public media manages complaint systems when the underlying material is controversial. The next step is the ABC's response and whether any standards change follows.
