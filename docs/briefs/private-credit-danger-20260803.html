Australia's private credit boom faces a stress test

Jon Adgemis's property collapse is now being used to examine how private credit has grown inside Australian commercial property. The risk matters because the debt sits outside the traditional bank system, yet it still shapes who absorbs losses when a big developer fails. The next questions are how deep the exposure runs, what creditors recover, and whether regulators need to tighten oversight.
