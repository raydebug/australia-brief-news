Northern prawn season opens with trawler numbers at a record low

Australia's northern prawn season has started with only 27 trawlers heading into the Northern Prawn Fishery, about half the fleet that operated last year. The smaller fleet matters because the fishery supports jobs, supply chains and port activity across the far north, and it also points to higher operating costs and a tighter season. The main question now is whether the lower number of boats will mean a stronger catch for the vessels that did sail or a thinner overall haul for the industry.
