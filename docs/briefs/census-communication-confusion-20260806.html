Some Australians are still waiting for Census instructions days before night

Reports from South Australia and other parts of the country say some households have not received Census instructions with only days to go before August 11. The ABS says mail is still moving and help is available, but local community workers say older residents are especially confused about the online form and paper options. It matters because a late or confusing rollout can hurt participation and make the count less reliable. The immediate issue is getting instructions out fast enough for people who still need them.
