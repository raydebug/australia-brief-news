Adani's Queensland mine posts big revenue but still pays no company tax

Adani’s Carmichael mine in Queensland has reported almost $1 billion in revenue but no company tax for the year. That matters because the mine was sold politically as a major jobs and revenue generator, yet the accounts show how costs and deductions can wipe out taxable profit. The next issue is whether critics push for tax changes or whether the company keeps operating within the current rules.
