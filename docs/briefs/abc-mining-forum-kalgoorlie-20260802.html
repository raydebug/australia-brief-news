Kalgoorlie forum ties mining growth to cooperation

At a mining forum in Kalgoorlie, speakers said the sector's next phase will depend on companies, governments and local communities working together. The discussion matters because Australia is still chasing investment in critical minerals, regional jobs and the infrastructure that keeps mining towns competitive. The next test is whether the talk turns into approvals, training and supply-chain spending over the coming year.
