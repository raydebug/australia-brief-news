Canberra tightens the levy that pushes big tech to pay for news

Canberra has tightened its plan for making large tech platforms pay for news, lifting the levy to 2.5 per cent of Australian digital ad revenue if companies do not strike deals with enough local publishers. The package also brings LinkedIn into scope and keeps the pressure on commercial negotiations with Australian outlets. It matters because the rules shape how journalism is funded and how much leverage the biggest platforms keep. Parliament now has to turn the draft into law, and both sides are already pushing for changes.
