A sharp cold front is set to bring snow, hail and damaging winds to the southeast

A cold front moving out of the Southern Ocean is forecast to sweep across Victoria, Tasmania, New South Wales and southern Queensland with heavy showers, hail, strong winds and snow on higher ground. Forecasters say the exact snow line and rainfall totals will vary by district, but the system could create travel, power and safety disruptions across several states. It matters because the change arrives quickly after milder weather and could affect a wide stretch of the east. The main uncertainty is how much moisture the front picks up as it crosses the country.
