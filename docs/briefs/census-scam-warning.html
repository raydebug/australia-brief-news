Scammers are trying to exploit Australia's census season

Fake census letters, emails and text messages are already circulating before the August count. The warning matters because scammers often borrow government branding to push people into clicking links or handing over personal details. The practical step now is simple: verify any census contact through official ABS channels before responding.
