Wildflowers are starting to bloom early in Western Australia's Midwest

The first orchids and other native blooms are beginning to appear in Western Australia's Midwest as the wildflower season gets underway. Early signs suggest a strong season after winter rain and cooler conditions helped set up the landscape. That matters because the bloom draw visitors, trade and attention to regional towns, while also showing how weather shapes fragile ecosystems. The next question is whether the season keeps building or dries out too quickly.
