ACCC inquiry follows claims about supermarket food labels

The ACCC has opened an inquiry after Four Corners raised claims about false country-of-origin labels and contaminated products. The regulator will test whether the conduct misled shoppers and broke consumer law. It matters because food labels shape trust in supermarket supply chains and buying decisions. The next step is whether the inquiry leads to enforcement or wider changes in the way products are labelled.
