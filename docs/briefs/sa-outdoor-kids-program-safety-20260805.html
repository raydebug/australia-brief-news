SA council shuts down an outdoor kids program over safety concerns

Mount Barker District Council has ordered Forest School SA to stop activities after raising serious safety concerns. The dispute matters because it sits where child safety, bushland learning and local regulation meet. The operator now has to answer the council's concerns before the program can restart. If the gap cannot be fixed, the shutdown could last longer or turn into formal enforcement.
