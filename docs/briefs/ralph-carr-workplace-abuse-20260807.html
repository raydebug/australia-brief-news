Former staff describe years of abuse under Ralph Carr

Former employees of Melbourne talent manager Ralph Carr say they worked through years of bullying, intimidation, and sexual harassment at his office. The allegations now sit alongside his separate rape conviction and a lifted suppression order, which has put fresh attention on how power was used in the entertainment industry. The accounts stretch back decades and come from women who were hired when they were still young and trying to break into the business. The next step is more legal and public fallout while Carr continues to deny the allegations.
