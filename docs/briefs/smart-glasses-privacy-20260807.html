Low-cost smart glasses draw fresh privacy scrutiny

Australia's privacy watchdog is being asked to look at the spread of low-cost smart glasses with built-in cameras. Officials say the devices can be useful, but they also make covert recording and harassment easier, especially in public places. The issue matters because the gear is getting cheaper before privacy rules have fully caught up. Regulators now have to decide whether existing law is enough or whether new limits are needed.
