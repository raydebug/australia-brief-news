Australia's housing downturn spreads as prices fall in more capitals

Home prices are falling across Australia, with Sydney and Melbourne leading the slide and Brisbane, Adelaide, Perth and Canberra also moving lower. New data cited by SBS, the Guardian and AAP puts the national median around $928,000, with values down about $19,000 since March. It matters because the downturn affects owners, investors and first-home buyers at the same time, and it may feed into the Reserve Bank's next decisions. The key question is whether the slide continues into spring.
