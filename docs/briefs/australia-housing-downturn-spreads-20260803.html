Australia's housing downturn spreads as prices fall in more capitals

Home prices are sliding across Australia, and July data show the weakness is no longer limited to Sydney and Melbourne. Cotality's figures put the national fall at 0.7 per cent, while Brisbane and Adelaide also moved lower and premium Sydney property stayed under pressure. It matters because falling values change the outlook for owners, investors and first-home buyers at the same time, and the trend could shape the Reserve Bank's next rate call. The main question is whether spring listings and interest-rate settings slow the slide or extend it.
