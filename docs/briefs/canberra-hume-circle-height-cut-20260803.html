Canberra planners trim Hume Circle's height cap after backlash

ACT planners have trimmed the maximum building height proposed for the Hume Circle precinct in south Canberra after strong community pushback. The change matters because it shapes how dense the area can become around a major transport junction and sets the tone for later housing decisions in the capital. The plan is still moving through consultation and final approval, so the design could change again before any construction starts.
