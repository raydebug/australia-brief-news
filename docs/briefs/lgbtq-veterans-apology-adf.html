LGBTQ+ veterans press for a formal apology over past ADF discrimination

A Senate committee is urging the federal government to apologise to LGBTQ+ veterans and service members who were punished under old Defence rules that barred homosexual personnel. David Gibson says the treatment he and others faced left lasting shame, and the report recommends record annotations, honourable-service certificates and formal recognition of the harm. It matters because those policies were only dropped in 1990, so the effects are still being felt by living veterans. The government now has to decide whether to go beyond the current bill and accept those restorative steps.
