A US-backed loan lifts a NSW scandium mine into focus

A US-backed financing package has put the Syerston scandium project in central-west New South Wales in the spotlight. About A$566 million is tied up in the plan, and the project matters because critical minerals now sit at the centre of supply chains, defence planning and industrial policy. The big question is how quickly the mine can move from promise to production and what it means for nearby jobs and infrastructure. The next stage is detailed financing, approvals and the pace of construction.
