St Kilda's little penguins are caught in the bird flu warning

Victoria's bird flu outbreak is now being tracked as a threat to St Kilda's little penguin colony, with officials warning the virus could take a heavy toll if it keeps moving through native wildlife. The story matters because the outbreak has moved beyond isolated detections and is starting to shape conservation planning across states. The next step is continued surveillance, plus decisions on whether more protection measures are needed for vulnerable colonies and coastal bird species.
