Tasmanian rescuers deal with three wilderness incidents in one day

Tasmanian search and rescue crews were called to three separate incidents across the island, including people in remote bush and alpine country. The day was busy enough to spread resources across different corners of the state, which is a reminder of how fast ordinary trips can turn into emergencies in winter conditions. The immediate priority is getting everyone to safety and checking whether anyone needs hospital treatment. Hikers are again being told to carry proper gear, plan ahead and treat remote tracks as serious terrain.
