Australia closes the nicotine-pouch loophole

Australia has tightened its rules so nicotine pouches can no longer be brought in or supplied through the old prescription pathway. Regulators say the products were spreading through retail back doors and online channels, with young people able to hide them easily in classrooms or other indoor spaces. That matters because nicotine pouches sit inside a broader illegal-tobacco market that health agencies have been trying to rein in for years. The key question now is whether border seizures and retail enforcement can keep pace with demand.
