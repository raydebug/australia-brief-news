High Court lets a secret abuse recording stand in a domestic violence case

Australia's High Court has upheld the use of a covert recording in a rape case, allowing a woman's phone recording of her husband's admissions to be used as evidence. The court said the recording protected her bodily autonomy and integrity after she feared being raped again. The ruling matters because it strengthens the legal path for victim-survivors who collect evidence of abuse in private. The next question is how far courts will apply that reasoning in other family violence cases and protection orders.
