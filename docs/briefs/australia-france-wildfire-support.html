Australia deploys crews to help France as wildfires spread across Europe

Australia is sending a Chinook helicopter, a Bell 412 aircraft and 24 NSW Rural Fire Service personnel to help France tackle major wildfires. The deployment comes while large fires continue to threaten homes and force evacuations in France and other parts of Europe. It matters because Australia is now contributing fire resources overseas even as it manages its own bushfire readiness. The next question is how quickly the team arrives and whether more support is needed as the European fire season drags on.
