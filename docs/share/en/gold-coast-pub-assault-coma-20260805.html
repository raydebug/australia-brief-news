Gold Coast assault victim remains in a coma as bail is refused

A man accused in a Gold Coast pub assault has been refused bail while the victim remains in a coma. The case is a reminder that one violent incident can quickly become both a criminal matter and a medical crisis. The accused now waits for his next court step while doctors continue to monitor the injured man in hospital. The key uncertainty is how much evidence builds before the next bail or committal hearing.
