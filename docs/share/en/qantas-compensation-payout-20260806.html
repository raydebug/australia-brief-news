Qantas workers are finally getting the remaining $38m payout

A Federal Court judge has ordered the remaining compensation from Qantas's 2020 outsourcing case to be paid out to affected ground crew. The money had been sitting in the court while the scheme was delayed, and Justice Michael Lee said the wait had gone on too long. It matters because the case remains one of Australia's biggest industrial rulings and it shows how compensation schemes can drag on long after the original job losses. The immediate step is distributing the funds, while the wider settlement process continues.
