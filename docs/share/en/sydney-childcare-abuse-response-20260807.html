Childcare abuse case widens as police identify more victims

Police have identified more alleged victims linked to former Sydney childcare worker Hamish Tait after using clothing images from seized devices to prompt fresh tips. The investigation now spans dozens of centres and remains focused on finding every child affected. The wider importance is that the case is adding pressure for a national childcare abuse register so warnings do not stay hidden inside one state or provider. The next step is further victim identification and any federal response on public naming rules.
