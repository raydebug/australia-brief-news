Australia's housing slide widens as recent buyers feel the squeeze

House prices fell again in July, and the slide is now reaching more of the country than just Sydney and Melbourne. AAP says about 34,000 first-home buyers who used the 5 per cent deposit scheme could face negative equity if the downturn deepens, while Cotality's figures still show a national monthly fall of 0.7 per cent. That matters because softer prices can help new buyers, but they also tighten the position of people who bought recently and may cool lending confidence. The next watchpoint is whether lower rates, more listings or policy changes steady the market.
