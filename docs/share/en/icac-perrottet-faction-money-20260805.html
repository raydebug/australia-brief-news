ICAC expands scrutiny of the Perrottet faction and its money trail

NSW corruption hearings have heard that Jean-Claude Perrottet did factional work while employed in a Liberal MP's office, while taped calls and texts raised new questions about donations and branch stacking. The issue matters because it goes to whether party politics, donor money and taxpayer-funded roles were mixed together inside the NSW Liberals. More witnesses are still due, and the inquiry could reshape how the party handles internal power and financing. No wrongdoing has been proven, but the pressure on senior figures is still building.
