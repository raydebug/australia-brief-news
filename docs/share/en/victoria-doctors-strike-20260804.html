Victorian hospital doctors set a strike date

Victorian public hospital doctors are set to stop work on August 13 in a pay dispute that now reaches some of Melbourne's biggest hospitals. The planned action runs from 12:30pm to 4:00pm and would affect the Royal Melbourne Hospital, the Royal Children's Hospital, the Royal Women's Hospital and nearby sites, while emergency doctors stay on duty. The dispute matters because it is the first protected industrial action at Victorian public hospitals in 20 years and could force non-urgent appointments to be rescheduled. The next question is whether the government and union can narrow the gap before the stoppage begins.
