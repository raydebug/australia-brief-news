Sunshine shop owner dies after a youth assault

Van Viet Truong, the owner of a grocery store in Melbourne's west, has died after an alleged attack by a group of teenagers in Sunshine. Police say the 60-year-old stepped in during a robbery attempt and was then badly hurt, and one teenager now faces a manslaughter charge. It matters because the case has moved from a street crime story to a homicide investigation and has sharpened concern about youth violence and trader safety. The next step is the court process and any further charges as investigators piece together what happened.
