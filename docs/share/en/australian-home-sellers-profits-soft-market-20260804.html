Most Australian home sellers still made gains despite a softer market

Fresh AAP data shows most home sellers are still pocketing a profit even as prices cool and concerns about negative equity rise. Fewer than 3% of houses sold in the first half of 2026 went for a loss, but the market is clearly less forgiving than it was a year ago. That matters because the housing slowdown is starting to hit both seller confidence and the shape of the next housing cycle. The open question is how long profits can hold up if prices keep easing.
