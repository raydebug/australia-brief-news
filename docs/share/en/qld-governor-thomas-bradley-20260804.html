Queensland appoints Thomas Bradley as governor

Queensland has named Supreme Court judge Thomas Bradley as its next governor, with a swearing-in set for November. Bradley will replace Jeannette Young and brings a long judicial background into the role. It matters because the governor is part constitutional, part symbolic, and the appointment says something about the government’s choice of public figure. The main unknown is how Bradley will use the post during a busy political term.
