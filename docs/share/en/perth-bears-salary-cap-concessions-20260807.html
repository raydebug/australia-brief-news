Perth Bears get salary-cap help to chase a marquee signing

The NRL has given the new Perth Bears salary-cap concessions so the expansion club can pursue at least one marquee signing. The move is meant to help the side build a competitive roster before it joins the competition. It matters because expansion clubs often need special rules to attract elite talent and stay viable from the start. The next step is which player the Bears target and how the league limits are applied.
