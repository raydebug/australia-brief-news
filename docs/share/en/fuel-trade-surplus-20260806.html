Fuel imports push Australia's trade surplus to its weakest level in years

Australia's goods trade surplus fell to about $1.1 billion in the June quarter after a surge in fuel and electric-vehicle imports. The immediate driver is the same Middle East fuel shock that forced governments and importers to secure extra cargoes, and that matters because the trade balance is now showing the cost of the supply response in hard numbers. The next thing to watch is whether fuel prices and import volumes keep weighing on the next ABS release.
