Heat 0

Wallabies brace for heat and typhoon conditions in Japan

Australia's rugby side faces a Test in Japan where extreme heat and a possible typhoon could affect selection, tactics and player safety. That matters because weather can change the tempo of the match, disrupt kicking plans and force last-minute changes to recovery and workload management. The main uncertainty now is how the forecast shifts before kickoff and whether officials need to adjust the schedule or preparations.
