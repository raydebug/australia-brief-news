Victoria watchdog says the Andrews government mishandled firefighter union dealings

Victoria's corruption watchdog has released its long-delayed Operation Richmond report and says senior figures in the former Andrews government engaged in serious and concerning conduct in dealings with the United Firefighters Union. The commission did not back the blackmail claims that had swirled around the case, but it said ministers and officials handled sensitive information and bargaining in ways that fell short of expected standards. That matters because the report goes to the heart of how public power was used in one of Victoria's most sensitive industrial fights. The next question is whether the findings lead to concrete reforms or just another round of political fallout.
