Sydney runway close call puts airport safety back under scrutiny

Airservices Australia says safety remains its top priority while the Australian Transport Safety Bureau investigates a runway near-miss in Sydney. The case matters because runway incidents can escalate quickly if aircraft get too close on the ground. The ATSB is still examining how often these close calls happen and what needs to change. For travellers, the issue is less about one incident than the systems meant to stop the next one.
