Newcastle climate protest charges are dropped after a long legal fight

More than 100 charges linked to a 2024 Newcastle coal-port blockade have been dropped or withdrawn, ending a major case against climate protesters. The decision follows a long legal fight and matters because it changes the risk calculation for future protest policing at one of Australia's most sensitive industrial sites. The key question now is whether police and prosecutors have shifted strategy after the earlier prosecutions lost momentum. Any appeal or follow-up legal action will be watched closely.
