Aged-care waits push families to a breaking point

Families say Australia's aged-care system is leaving too many older people waiting too long for help, with pressure building on providers and on relatives trying to arrange care. The issue matters because it turns a long-running policy problem into immediate stress for families who are already exhausted. What happens next depends on whether governments and operators can lift capacity fast enough to match demand.
