Toxic sulphur fire in Port Adelaide forces evacuations

A fire in an industrial shed at Port Adelaide released toxic smoke after it broke out among around 30,000 tonnes of sulphur. Emergency crews ordered people within 1 kilometre to leave, and two workers were taken to hospital as the blaze was brought under control. It matters because the smoke posed a local health risk and the site contained a large hazardous chemical load. Investigators still need to determine what started the fire and whether more environmental checks are needed.
