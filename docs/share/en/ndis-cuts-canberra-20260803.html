Canberra disability recipient fears looming NDIS cuts

Nicci Carlson, a Canberra NDIS recipient, says looming cuts to the scheme leave vulnerable people feeling like scapegoats during the cost-of-living squeeze. The case matters because the debate is no longer abstract: real support, eyesight and daily independence are on the line. The next step is whether the federal reforms protect people with high needs while still slowing spending growth.
