Wallabies edge Japan after red-card drama

Australia scraped past Japan 35-32 in Osaka after a red card left the Wallabies under pressure for long spells. The result matters because Les Kiss has started his coaching tenure with a win, but the performance also showed how quickly discipline problems can swing a Test. The next questions are whether the side settles faster and how much work remains before the return meeting in Townsville.
