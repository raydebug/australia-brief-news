South Australian hospital corridor care prompts inquiry calls

Doctors and nurses in South Australia are warning that patients are still being treated in hospital corridors, which suggests the pressure inside the system has not eased. The issue matters because corridor care can slow treatment, strain staff and raise safety risks for people waiting on proper beds. The next step is whether the state or hospital managers order a formal review and move to free up space before more winter pressure hits.
