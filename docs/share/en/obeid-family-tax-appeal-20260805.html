Obeid family tax fight could stretch out over a coal windfall dispute

The family of former NSW Labor powerbroker Eddie Obeid is still fighting the tax office over a $30 million windfall from a coal project sale. Eleven relatives or spouses are trying to delay the final appeal in a case that has already dragged on for years. It matters because the dispute has become a long test of how the tax office treats large, politically sensitive gains. The next step is whether the court lets the appeal move on now or gives the family more time.
