South Sydney survives Parramatta's furious second-half surge

South Sydney beat Parramatta 28-24 after leading 28-0 at half-time and then hanging on through a huge Eels fightback. Alex Johnston scored three tries, and Cody Walker's late 40-20 helped seal the result. It matters because the Rabbitohs looked in control early but still had to defend their place in the top eight when the game tightened. The next step is whether they can keep that composure when future matches turn against them.
