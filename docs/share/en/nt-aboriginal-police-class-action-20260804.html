NT Aboriginal police officers launch racism class action

Current and former Aboriginal community police officers in the Northern Territory have launched a class action over claims of racial abuse, discrimination and underpayment. The case matters because it turns long-running workplace complaints into a major legal test for the NT Police Force and the territory government. It also raises the question of how much of the alleged culture can be proven in federal court. The next step is the court timetable and whether the parties move toward settlement or trial.
