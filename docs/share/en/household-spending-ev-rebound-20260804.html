Household spending rises as EV purchases rebound

Australian household spending rose 0.8 per cent in June, helped by stronger new-car buying and a rebound in electric vehicle sales. The numbers matter because they show consumers still spending even with cost-of-living pressure, and because transport choices are now moving the monthly figures. Air travel also recovered after earlier weakness, which suggests some households are still willing to spend on bigger-ticket items. The key question is whether that momentum lasts once fuel and broader price pressures settle.
