Tasmania's Shyanne-Lee murder case gets an April 2027 trial date

The court has set an April 2027 trial for Christopher Mark Jordan, who is accused of murdering 14-year-old Shyanne-Lee Tatnell. The hearing is expected to run for about five weeks, almost four years after she disappeared near Launceston and after her remains were found near Nabowla. It matters because the case has moved through a long pre-trial process and now has a clear date. The next stage is further case management before the trial opens next year.
