Adelaide's Royal Show drops poultry exhibits as bird flu cases climb

The Royal Adelaide Show will not run its bird and poultry exhibits after H5 bird flu cases kept rising in South Australia. Organisers are trimming the animal displays to reduce the chance of spreading infection while the event still goes ahead. That matters because the outbreak is now changing public events as well as wildlife monitoring. The next question is whether more agricultural shows and farm-linked events will face similar restrictions if cases keep climbing.
