Native wildflower plantings are spreading through Australian cities

Native wildflower meadows are showing up in parks, roadside strips and other council spaces across Australia. The shift matters because these plantings are meant to handle heat better, cost less to maintain and support more local insects and birds than a plain lawn. The question now is whether more councils copy the approach and make it part of standard city landscaping. If that happens, the change could become both an aesthetic and environmental update.
