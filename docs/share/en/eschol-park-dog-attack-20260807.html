Police probe a fatal dog attack at an Eschol Park property

Police are investigating the death of a 23-year-old man found with severe injuries at a property in Eschol Park in south-west Sydney. Officers say he may have been an intruder, and dogs were removed from the site while detectives and the coroner continue their inquiries. It matters because investigators still do not know whether this was a fatal attack, a trespass case or something else entirely. The key question is what happened in the moments before he died.
