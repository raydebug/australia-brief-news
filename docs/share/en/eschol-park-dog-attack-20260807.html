Eschol Park death remains under police scrutiny after dog attack claims

Police are still investigating the death of a 23-year-old man found with severe injuries at a property in Eschol Park in south-west Sydney. Officers say he may have been an intruder, and dogs have been removed from the site while detectives and the coroner work through the circumstances. The case matters because investigators are still trying to determine whether this was a fatal dog attack, a trespass death or something else entirely. The next step is the forensic and legal review that will establish how he died.
