More Australians are planning a backup career path

7NEWS says more Australians are thinking about a second career path as work expectations shift and AI changes the job market. One example is a 22-year-old heading back to study information technology to widen his options, while survey data suggests many workers are unsure their first career choice will still fit in future. It matters because skills updating is becoming a personal risk as well as an economic one, especially for younger workers and people trying to pivot. The question now is whether training systems can keep up with that sense of uncertainty.
