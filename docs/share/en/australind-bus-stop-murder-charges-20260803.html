Two men face murder charges over Australind bus stop death

Two men in their 30s have been charged with murder after a man was found dead with critical injuries at a bus stop in Australind, south of Bunbury. Police say the man was discovered before dawn on Sunday and later died in hospital. It matters because the case has triggered a homicide investigation in a small WA community and could lead to more evidence-gathering or court action. Detectives are still piecing together what happened.
