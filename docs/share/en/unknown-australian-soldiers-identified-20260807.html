Unknown Australian soldiers identified decades after wartime deaths

The Australian Army's war-casualty identification work has named two soldiers who had been buried as unknown Australians for decades. One died in France in 1918, and the other was lost in Papua New Guinea in 1942, and both graves will now receive named headstones. It matters because it gives families a final answer and shows the identification program is still closing historical gaps. More old remains may still be matched as the team keeps testing records and burial sites.
