Police treat a Melbourne arson attack as a possible terror case

Australian counter-terror police have launched a landmark investigation after a firebombing was treated as a possible terrorism offence. Operation Braehead is examining alleged pro-Palestinian motives behind the attack, which makes it the first post-October 7 terror probe of its kind in Australia. It matters because the outcome may shape how police handle politically charged arson cases. The next stage is identifying suspects and testing whether the motive claim holds up.
