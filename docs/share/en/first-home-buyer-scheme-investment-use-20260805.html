First-home guarantee scheme draws scrutiny over investment conversions

A Guardian analysis says nearly 1,500 homes bought through Australia's 5% first-home guarantee have later been converted into investment properties. The report says purchases surged after income caps were removed, with monthly take-up climbing past 5,600. That matters because the scheme was meant to help owner-occupiers, not to become a back door for investors. The next question is whether Canberra tightens compliance or keeps the settings unchanged.
