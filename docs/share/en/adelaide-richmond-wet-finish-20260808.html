Adelaide holds Richmond off in a rain-soaked finish

Adelaide beat Richmond by nine points at the end of a wet, scrappy night at Adelaide Oval. Izak Rankine and Taylor Walker provided the decisive moments, and the win keeps the Crows in the finals picture. The result matters because they needed a hard-earned response in poor conditions, not just another comfortable home-game performance. The next question is whether they can turn that into a stronger run over the final rounds.
