Melbourne locals rally after bail is granted in a shopkeeper death case

Residents in Melbourne's west are preparing to rally after three youths linked to a shopkeeper's death were granted bail. The case has become a test of how the justice system handles youth crime, community anger and the family's grief after a 60-year-old shop owner died confronting alleged shoplifters. The next stage is the protest itself and the court process, which will now draw even more scrutiny.
