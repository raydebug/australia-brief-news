Australian lithium miners push to capture more battery value onshore

Australian lithium miners are trying to move beyond the old dig-and-ship model and keep more of the battery supply chain in Australia. The push matters because China still dominates processing, so onshore refineries could lift local value and make exports less exposed to one overseas buyer. The hurdle is cost and scale, because processing plants need capital, customers and stable policy support. The next step is whether the first projects can turn from ambition into commercial output.
