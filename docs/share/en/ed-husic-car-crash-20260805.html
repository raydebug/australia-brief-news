Ed Husic is recovering after a Sydney motorway crash

Labor MP Ed Husic was hit from behind by a truck on Sydney's M7 and taken to hospital with fractures, abrasions and a concussion. He has since been discharged and says he will take a short break from parliamentary duties while he recovers. That matters because the crash happened on a major commuter route and police are still checking the circumstances. The main next step is his recovery and the inquiry into how the collision happened.
