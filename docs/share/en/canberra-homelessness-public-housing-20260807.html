Canberra's housing wait pushes more people into homelessness

ACT services say more people are seeking help because the city’s public-housing queue leaves them with nowhere affordable to go. Recent reporting says there are about 3,600 approved applicants waiting, with priority cases still taking months and standard housing stretching beyond five years. The issue is not just emergency shelter; it is how long families and single people can stay stable before a public home becomes available. If the supply does not move, local services keep absorbing the pressure.
