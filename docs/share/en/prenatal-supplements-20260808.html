Health experts push back on extra pregnancy supplement claims

Australian pregnancy guidelines still point to folic acid, iodine and iron as the core supplements for most expectant parents. Health experts are warning that social media is now pushing extra products that may do nothing or create avoidable harm. That matters because pregnancy advice is easy to distort and families can end up buying into marketing instead of evidence. The next step is whether clinicians, regulators and platform rules do more to cut through the noise.
