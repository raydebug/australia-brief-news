Victoria reshapes cabinet with an AI minister and fewer infrastructure portfolios

Victoria's new cabinet shifts transport infrastructure and Suburban Rail Loop responsibilities out of the old structure, while the premier also creates an AI minister role. The change follows last week's leadership switch and a wave of resignations, so several portfolios are being rebalanced at once. It matters because the reshuffle changes who drives major projects and signals where the government wants its next policy push to land. The main question is whether the new team can settle quickly enough to keep the agenda moving.
