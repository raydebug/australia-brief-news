EY says AI could lift productivity and add jobs across Australia

New EY modelling suggests artificial intelligence could lift Australia's productivity by as much as 2.4 per cent over the next decade and support about 44,000 extra jobs. The forecast also points to a large GDP gain if businesses adopt the technology and reskill workers fast enough. It matters because Australia has been stuck in a long productivity slowdown, and the debate now reaches living standards, energy use and the rules for data centres. The open question is whether policy can keep pace and whether the gains spread beyond the biggest companies.
