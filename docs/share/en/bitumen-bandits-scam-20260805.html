A bogus bitumen crew leaves a Riverland family out of pocket

A Riverland businessman says a fake road crew charged nearly $10,000 for work that left his driveway damaged and unfinished. The warning is simple: doorstep contractors can move fast, especially in regional areas, and the cost of a bad hire can be far higher than the quote. The next step is whether consumer warnings and enforcement can stop the same scam from spreading.
