Canva's AI spending forces a rare revenue reset

Canva says the shift to AI has cost more than expected, and it has cut revenue targets as a result. That matters because the company is one of Australia's biggest tech names, and its spending choices affect hiring, product timing and investor confidence. The key question now is whether Canva can absorb the higher bill without slowing growth for too long.
