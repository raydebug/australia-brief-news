Long-term missing-person cases leave families without support

For more than a decade, Cathy Wood has searched for answers about her father's disappearance, and her case shows how long-term missing-person cases can leave families with little formal support. Richard Wood vanished after his boat was found moored in Moreton Bay, and police called off the search after eight days. The wider issue matters because thousands of Australians are living with unresolved losses and the gap in counselling and case follow-up can stretch on for years. The next question is whether states and the Commonwealth expand support for families caught in that limbo.
