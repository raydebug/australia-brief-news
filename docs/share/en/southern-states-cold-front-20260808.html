Southern states brace for a fast-moving cold front with rain, snow and flood risk

A strong cold front is moving across southern Australia and is expected to bring damaging winds, heavy rain and some flood risk over the weekend. The Bureau of Meteorology has warnings in place for western, southern and south-eastern states, with snow possible on higher ground. The system matters because it could disrupt travel, lift river levels and cause road and power problems before conditions ease early next week.
