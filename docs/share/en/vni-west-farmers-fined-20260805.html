Victorian farmers are fined after blocking VNI West survey access

Victorian landholders opposing the VNI West transmission project have been fined after refusing access for survey work. The dispute is about whether authorised officers should be allowed onto private property so environmental checks and planning can continue. It matters because VNI West is part of the state's renewable rollout, but land access fights can slow construction and deepen tensions between rural communities and the grid build-out. The next step is whether the dispute moves further through the courts or pushes ministers to rethink how the project is handled.
