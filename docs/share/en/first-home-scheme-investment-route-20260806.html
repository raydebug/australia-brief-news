Australia's 5% deposit scheme is being used as an investment route

A new look at the federal 5% deposit scheme says nearly 1,500 homes bought through it ended up as investment properties between 2020 and May 2026. The program was designed to help first-home buyers by letting them borrow 95 per cent of a home's value with a government guarantee, but the data now shows some higher-income buyers have also used it. That matters because the scheme is still expanding, house-price pressure is uneven, and policymakers need to know whether the guarantee is helping genuine buyers get in or helping investors take on more debt. The next question is whether Canberra tightens eligibility, disclosure or monitoring rules.
