Coronial inquest probes Albury hospital transfer delays after Kate Manley's death

A NSW coronial inquest has begun into the care Kate Manley received before her death at Albury Base Hospital. Evidence will examine whether transfer delays and treatment inside the mental health ward met the standards expected at the time. The case matters because hospital handover problems can turn a treatable crisis into a fatal one, especially when a patient is already under close supervision. The inquest will run over the next two weeks and may still hear from clinicians and family witnesses.
