Brisbane crash footage sharpens a fatal bus case

New CCTV from inside a Brisbane bus shows the moment a car became airborne and slammed into the vehicle, leaving its front end perched on a bus seat. The footage matters because it gives a clearer picture of a crash already linked to a pedestrian death and a court appeal. The next step is the appeal process and any further scrutiny of how the collision unfolded.
